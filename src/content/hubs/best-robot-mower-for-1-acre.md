One acre of **grass** (not lot size) sits at the awkward middle of the robot-mower market: many machines top out under 0.5–0.75 acre, and only a handful of published listings on this site are rated at **about 1 acre or above**. This page curates those listings and the closest undersize fits. It is directory yard-fit, not a ranked “best 1-acre mower” badge.

Measure the lawn. If you are actually at 0.6 acre, a 0.75–0.87 acre card may be enough. If you are at a messy 1.1 acre with islands and beds, prefer models that publish a lower **irregular** capacity — or step up.

---

## Size / coverage framing

On this site, `coverageAcres` is the manufacturer’s **recommended** work area from the listing (see the [publisher README](https://github.com/ClaytonHeath/best-robot-mowers/blob/main/README.md)). Marketing labels on the same PDP sometimes claim a higher ceiling. When those conflict, listings already document both — and this hub follows the **recommended** number for yard-fit.

**Rough bands for this page:**

| Band | How this hub treats it |
| --- | --- |
| **~0.75–0.9 acre** | Closest undersize / near-miss — OK if your measured grass is under 1.0 and layout is simple |
| **~1.0–1.5 acre** | Primary 1-acre fit from the live catalog |
| **Above ~1.5 acre** | Estate / large systematic — overkill for a true 1.0 acre but listed so you do not undersize upward |

**Catalog honesty:** Only a few published SKUs hit **≥1.0 acre** recommended coverage. The small- and mid-yard shelf is much thicker. If few models fit, that is the market state of this directory as of 18 Sep 2026 — not a reason to invent acreage.

## Models at or above ~1 acre (primary fits)

### [Segway Navimow X430](/mowers/segway-navimow-x430/)

- **Slope:** 84% · **Drive:** AWD · **Nav:** RTK + vision · wire-free
- **Cut:** 17 in dual-disc · **Price:** $2,249 US cart (2 Sep 2026)
- **Who it’s for (listing):** Hilly or uneven lawns up to 1 acre that want wire-free AWD and a wide deck.
- **What breaks the deal:** Yard over an acre (listing points at X450, which is **not** a published page on this site); paths narrower than the **24-inch** body.

### [Husqvarna Automower 435 iQ AWD](/mowers/husqvarna-automower-435-iq-awd/)
- **Coverage:** **1.3 acre** systematic / **0.9 acre irregular**
- **Slope:** 70% in-lawn / 50% boundary · **Drive:** AWD · **Nav:** EPOS RTK · wire-free
- **Price:** ~$4,799 street (1 Sep 2026 range on listing)
- **Who it’s for (listing):** Steep ~1.3-acre systematic lawns (~0.9 if irregular) that need AWD and virtual boundaries.
- **What breaks the deal:** Irregular layouts over ~0.9 acre; need for hose-wash underside (IPX4); refusing the EPOS reference-station install story.

### [Husqvarna Automower 440 iQ](/mowers/husqvarna-automower-440-iq/)
- **Coverage:** **2 acre** systematic / **1 acre irregular**
- **Slope:** 45% in-lawn / 15% boundary · **Nav:** EPOS RTK · wire-free (not AWD)
- **Price:** ~$4,299 street (30 Aug 2026)
- **Who it’s for (listing):** Up to 2-acre systematic mowing; about 1 acre if the layout is irregular.
- **What breaks the deal:** Messy acre-plus when you only looked at the 2-acre headline; steep banks better served by AWD SKUs; hills over 45%.

### [Segway Navimow X390](/mowers/segway-navimow-x390/)
- **Coverage:** **2.5 acres** recommended
- **Slope:** 50% work / 25% boundary · **Drive:** Rear-wheel drive · **Nav:** RTK + vision · wire-free
- **Price:** $4,999 MSRP on listing ($4,499 official store noted 26 Aug 2026)
- **Who it’s for (listing):** Large, relatively open lawns up to 2.5 acres that do not need AWD.
- **What breaks the deal:** Steep wet hills (RWD); second antenna cost if one base cannot cover; overkill budget if the lawn is a clean 1.0 acre and X430 AWD fits.

### [Yarbo Lawn Mower Pro](/mowers/yarbo-lawn-mower-pro/)
- **Coverage:** **6 acres** max mowing size (also publishes 1.7 acres per day on the listing)
- **Slope:** 70% · **Nav:** RTK + vision · wire-free · modular Core + deck
- **Price:** $5,499
- **Who it’s for (listing):** Very large wire-free yards toward that 6-acre ceiling.
- **What breaks the deal:** You wanted a compact one-piece suburban robot; weight (~216 lb) and modular system cost.

## What usually breaks a 1-acre deal

1. **Sizing to marketing acres, not recommended acres** (Genie 3000 / 1000 pattern above).
2. **Ignoring Husqvarna irregular capacity** — 440 iQ’s 2-acre systematic headline becomes **1 acre** irregular; 435 iQ AWD’s 1.3 becomes **0.9**.
3. **Slope vs coverage mismatch** — LUBA 3 AWD 1500 has 80% slope but only **0.37 acre**; it is not a 1-acre hill machine in this catalog.
4. **Passage width / body width** — X430’s 24-inch body is an explicit listing limit.
5. **Wire appetite** — 435X AWD can cover ~0.9 acre on hills but requires physical wire; acre-class iQ / Navimow / Yarbo stay wire-free at higher price.
6. **Unpublished larger siblings** — Listings mention Pro 5000, X450, larger LUBA SKUs as *not this page*. This directory only links **published** slugs. Do not assume those siblings are for sale here until they have listings.

## Thin catalog above 1 acre — what to do

As of 18 Sep 2026, published **≥1.0 acre recommended** cards are essentially:

1. Navimow X430 (1.0)
2. Automower 435 iQ AWD (1.3 systematic)
3. Automower 440 iQ (2.0 systematic / 1.0 irregular)
4. Navimow X390 (2.5)
5. Yarbo Lawn Mower Pro (6.0 max)

If none of those fit budget, drive type, or install constraints:

- Drop to **0.75–0.87** near-misses only if the measured lawn is actually under 1.0.
- Use homepage filters (`?coverage=0.5-1.5` / `over-1.5`) on [bestlawnrobots.com](/).
- Do not buy a 0.5-acre card ([Dreame A3 AWD 2000](/mowers/dreame-a3-awd-2000/), [Automower 410 iQ](/mowers/husqvarna-automower-410-iq/)) for a true acre and hope runtime schedules fix capacity — the listing coverage field is the yard-fit gate.

Specs and prices come from those listings (official manufacturer pages and live carts as of each listing’s `updated` date). There is no “#1 robot mower 2026,” no invented scores, and no fake testimonials.

Start with **yard size**, then **slope**, then whether you will bury a boundary loop. Open each listing for the caveat. Tick cards on the [homepage](/) or the [wire-free hub](/wire-free/) if you want side-by-side spec rows.

Coverage figures are the manufacturer’s **recommended** work area unless the listing says otherwise. Some SKUs (Anthbot Genie especially) market a higher ceiling than the recommended figure — use the listing’s recommended number for yard-fit.

---

## How to use this roundup

1. Measure grass area (not lot size).
2. Check the steepest grade **inside** the work area — and separately any grade at the boundary or along a driveway edge.
3. Decide: wire-free (RTK / LiDAR / vision) vs. traditional boundary wire.
4. Match a section below, then open 2–3 listing pages before you spend $1.5k–$5k+.

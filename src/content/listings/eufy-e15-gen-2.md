## What it is

The eufy Robot Lawn Mower E15 Gen 2 is a wire-free, camera-only robot mower sold on eufy's official US store at eufy.com. This listing covers the **Gray E15 Gen 2**: Shopify product **10321163616442**, variant **50692451958970**, SKU **T280L1A1**. It is not the listed [eufy E15](/mowers/eufy-e15/) (SKU T28801A1), which the same page sells alongside it.

eufy calls the navigation **eufy TrueVision**, with an upgraded **V-FSD**. Dual cameras and onboard AI map the yard and plan the route, with **no boundary wire and no RTK station**. eufy says the mower maps and mows at the same time from the first run ("Zero Mapping Wait"). It says the maps are 25% more complete, and it can cover front and back zones up to 60 m apart without being carried.

From the **E15 Gen2 (T280L) user guide** linked from the US product page (§2.4 Specifications):

- Recommended mowing area: **1,000 m² (0.25 acre)**
- Maximum slope: **18°**. The guide prints "18° (40%)", but 18° is about 32%, so this listing uses only the degree figure.
- Cutting width: **8 in (203 mm)**. Cutting height: **1–3 in (25–75 mm)**.
- Mower: **23.7 × 15.5 × 12.5 in**, **26 lbs**, **IPX6**
- Base station: **28.6 × 18.9 × 17.8 in** (15.7 in without the garage), **13 lbs**, **IPX6**
- Battery: **93.24 Wh** (22.2 V, 4,200 mAh). Mowing time per charge: **90–110 min**. Charging time: **90–110 min**.
- Noise: **56.0 dB(A)** sound pressure (LpA) and **66.30 dB** sound power (LWA)
- GPS anti-theft accuracy: 20–30 m. Wi-Fi 2.4/5 GHz plus LTE.
- Warranty: **2 years** on the mower, base station, power adapter and battery pack. None on blades.

**In the box (user guide §2.1):** the mower, base station, garage, power adapter, blade kit and grease.

## Who it's for

Owners of a **small, flat lawn up to a quarter acre** who want nothing in the ground and no antenna to place. The page's quick-start notes say setup takes about 5 minutes and the mower maps as it mows. It handles split front and back lawns up to 60 m (65.6 yd) apart with a multi-zone pathway. Each base station supports one map.

Check eufy's lawn checklist first:
- Slopes must be **18° or less**; steeper areas need no-go zones.
- Grass should be under **3.5 in** before the first mow.
- Holes and uneven ground need filling.
- eufy doesn't recommend it for **Zoysia or St. Augustine**.

The page also says to use 4G after setup if Wi-Fi coverage is weak.

## Standout details

- **Navigation and avoidance:** TrueVision pure-vision mapping with no RTK. eufy says AI semantic segmentation identifies 500+ obstacle types. The camera FOV is 96° horizontal and 80° vertical; the page warns of blind spots at the sides and rear.
- **Edges:** ride-on-edge mowing, which eufy rates to within **5.9 in (15 cm)** of the edge, with app-set edge distance. The FAQ notes grass within about **4 in** of walls may still need hand trimming.
- **Runtime:** **93.24 Wh** pack, **90–110 min** mowing and **90–110 min** charging. The FAQ puts one charge at **150–270 m² (0.037–0.067 acre)**. The mower resumes unfinished jobs after charging.
- **Security integration:** through eufy's app Automation feature, linked eufy cameras or doorbells can pause mowing when a person, pet or vehicle is detected. The app also offers GPS + 4G anti-theft and remote camera view.
- **Lawn care app:** the "Green Master" assistant suggests mowing settings by lawn type, climate zone and weather.
- **Build:** **26 lbs**, **IPX6** on both the mower and the base, **56 dB(A)** sound pressure.
- **Purchase terms (19 Oct 2026):** **$1,799.99** in a guest cart with no discount code, free shipping, tax at checkout. A paid Plus membership adds $179.99 off, which isn't included here.

## Honest verdict

At **$1,799.99**, the E15 Gen 2 is a camera-only mower for a flat quarter acre. There's no wire or antenna to install, it maps as it mows, and it has IPX6, GPS + 4G anti-theft and eufy camera integration.

Both have the same cut, height range, battery, runtime, charge time and slope limit. The Gen 2 adds 0.05 acre of rating, skips the separate mapping run, detects more obstacle types and links to eufy cameras.

The limits are the 18° slope cap, a small battery that covers about 150–270 m² per charge, a grass-type exclusion that rules out much of the US South, and a 3.5 in grass-height starting limit. Buy the E15 Gen 2 if your lawn is a flat quarter acre or less of cool-season grass. Choose an RTK or AWD machine if the yard is sloped or bigger.

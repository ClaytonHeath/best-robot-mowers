## What it is

The **Yarbo Y40P Lawn Mower Pro** is Yarbo's newer **Y40P Core** sold with the **Lawn Mower Pro Module** as one US SKU, **YAR-Y40P-LM** (variant 45426696126561). On 3 Nov 2026 (AWST) the official store listed it at **$7,099.00** against a **$7,599.00** compare-at, and a guest cart accepted it at that price in USD with no discount.

Like the [Yarbo Lawn Mower Pro](https://www.bestlawnrobots.com/mowers/yarbo-lawn-mower-pro/), it is a modular yard robot: a tracked Core that drives a separate mowing deck. Yarbo's own FAQ explains the difference. Both use the same Lawn Mower Pro Module with dual **300W** cutting motors. The Y40P changes the **Core**, which has a **brushless hub motor drive** rated at up to **1.2 m/s** against 0.65 m/s on the Y40.

The official US product page publishes these figures:

- Up to **0.3 acre** (1,214 m²) per charge, **2 acres** (8,090 m²) per day, and **7 acres** (28,000 m²) with twice-weekly mowing.
- Slopes up to **70% (35°)**.
- Up to **120 minutes** runtime per charge.
- Noise **under 60 dB**.
- Cutting height **0.8–4.0 in (2–10 cm)**. Dual cutting discs, each with five razor blades, plus optional SK85 straight blades.
- Navigation: **NetRTK** with stereo vision, odometry and an IMU. It is wire-free and needs no rooftop base station.

The box includes the Y40P Core, the Lawn Mower Pro Module, a docking station, a Y Series battery, a wired charger, a tow hitch, two RTK Antenna Pro units for the rover, a HaLow antenna, and spare blades.

## Who it's for

Owners of multi-acre properties, especially hilly or uneven ones, who mow often enough that the higher daily rate matters. It suits sites with network coverage for NetRTK. Yarbo pitches the Y40P as needing no base station to set up, and as easy to move to a new yard.

It is the wrong buy if your lawn is 6 acres or less. The Y40 [Yarbo Lawn Mower Pro](https://www.bestlawnrobots.com/mowers/yarbo-lawn-mower-pro/) uses the same deck and was $5,399 on its listing (23 Oct 2026). It is also wrong if you want a single sealed mower body, or a published weight and IP rating before you buy.

## Standout details

- **Price:** $7,099.00 in the official US cart on 3 Nov 2026 (AWST), on sale from $7,599.00. Y40P bundles that add Blower, Trimmer or Snow Blower Gen 2 modules run **$8,099–$11,399**. Those are separate SKUs and are not covered here.
- **Core upgrade:** a brushless hub-motor drive rated at **1.2 m/s**, against 0.65 m/s for the Y40. Yarbo claims smoother motion and better stability on uneven ground.
- **Capacity:** 0.3 acre per charge, 2 acres per day and 7 acres per week. Yarbo's FAQ gives the Y40 figures as 0.25 acre, 1.7 acres and 6 acres.
- **Cutting:** the same Lawn Mower Pro deck with dual 300W motors and a 0.8–4.0 in height range. Optional straight blades come in the box.
- **Not published on the Y40P page:** weight, IP rating and cutting width. They are left out here rather than copied from the Y40 version.
- **Warranty:** a 2-year limited warranty, with an optional 3-year extension, and a 30-day return policy.

## Honest verdict

At **$7,099** (3 Nov 2026), the Y40P is Yarbo's biggest-capacity mower-only SKU. It is worth it if you really need 7 acres a week or the faster, smoother Core on rough ground. Most buyers with 6 acres or less get the same cutting deck for less in the Y40 version. Both are modular, premium-priced systems. Check NetRTK coverage at your address before you buy.

## What it is

Husqvarna Automower 435X AWD is a residential, all-wheel-drive robot mower for lawns up to **0.9 acre (±20%)**. The US SKU is **967 85 33-05**. Husqvarna lists the boundary type as **physical wire**. This is not the 435 iQ AWD: no EPOS virtual mapping, no satellite boundary. A buried (or pegged) perimeter loop defines the work area.

The box includes the mower, charging station, power supply, 33 ft low-voltage cable, and 6 extra blades. **Loop wire and stakes are not included.** US dealer carts were about **$2,999.99** on 27 Aug 2026; husqvarna.com does not publish a list price.

## Who it's for

Yards that are medium in area and awkward in terrain — slopes, obstacles, a pivoting AWD chassis — and where the owner will install perimeter wire (or pay for a professional install). Official slope is **70% inside the work area** and **50% at the boundary wire**. It is the wrong machine if you will not bury wire, or if the grass is actually over about an acre once you measure.

## Honest verdict

This is the wire-era Automower for a hilly ~0.9-acre lawn: AWD, 70% in-lawn slope, no rooftop RTK mast. The catch is the install — physical wire is required, and the kit in the box does not include that wire or the stakes. IPX4 is weather protection, not a hose-down rating, and 8.7 in of blade is a small deck for almost an acre. If you want virtual boundaries, that is a different SKU (435 iQ AWD).

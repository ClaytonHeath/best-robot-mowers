## What it is

Anthbot **M5 Pro** is the 500 m² model in Anthbot's **M PRO Series**. On the official US store it is the **M5 PRO** package (Shopify product **15415826121006**, variant **67889017880878**, SKU **105010114**) at **$1,099.00**, with no compare-at price. On 1 Nov 2026 (AWST) the page showed it as available with no preorder label, and a guest cart took it at **$1,099.00 USD**. When checked on 29 Oct 2026, the same page was titled "(Preorder)" with shipping expected from 27 Oct 2026. Navigation is **HoloSense Quad-Fusion Positioning**: 360° LiDAR, RTK/NetRTK and AI vision, with no perimeter wire. Anthbot says LiDAR keeps the mower positioned if RTK drops out. It shares its chassis and spec sheet with the listed **M9 Pro** (`anthbot-m9-pro`, 1000 m²). It is a different SKU from the listed **M5** (`anthbot-m5`, RTK + dual vision) and **M5 LiDAR** (`anthbot-m5-lidar`, LiDAR + dual vision, no RTK). The **M5 PRO + Garage M** package ($1,199) on the same page is not this listing.

## Who it's for

The M5 Pro suits lawns up to Anthbot's published **recommended mowing area of 500 m² (1/8 acre)**, especially ones where trees or buildings would leave a satellite-only mower without a fix. Anthbot markets app-only setup on NetRTK ("no wire, no RTK station, 3 min"). The **65 cm (25.6 in)** narrowest passage fits side yards, and the official max slope is **24° / 45%**. It is the wrong pick if your lawn is over 500 m² (look at the M9 Pro instead), or if the cheaper M5 LiDAR or base M5 already handles your yard.

- **Box contents vs. marketing:** The shared M5 Pro / M9 Pro "What's in the Box" list still includes an **RTK reference station**, mounting pole and tripod stake, even though Anthbot says you can skip installing the station when you use NetRTK.

- **Cut:** It has a **20 cm (7.9 in)** blade disc and mows in U-shaped paths. Cutting height is **3–7 cm (1.2–2.8 in)**.
- **Build:** Noise is **≤ 58 dB** and the mower is rated **IPX6**. Anthbot publishes no weight, battery capacity or drive layout for the M Pro series, so this listing leaves those fields out.

## Honest verdict

The M5 Pro is Anthbot's full-fusion mower for small lawns: **LiDAR + RTK/NetRTK + vision** for up to **500 m² (1/8 acre)**, with a **45%** slope limit and a **7.9 in** disc, at **$1,099** (1 Nov 2026). Buy it if your small yard is shaded or cluttered enough to need both LiDAR and RTK. If one sensor type is enough, the M5 LiDAR covers the same area for $340 less ($759 on 11 Nov 2026). If you are close to 500 m², spend the extra $100 on the M9 Pro for twice the rated area.

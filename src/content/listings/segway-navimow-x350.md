## What it is

The Segway Navimow X350 is the 1.5-acre model in Navimow's **X3 series**, sold on the official US store at navimow.com. This listing is Shopify product **7975880392841**, variant **43296343949449**, SKU **AA.12.03.06.0001**. It sits between the listed [X330](/mowers/segway-navimow-x330/) (1 acre) and [X390](/mowers/segway-navimow-x390/) (2.5 acres).

Navigation is **EFLS 3.0**. Navimow's US manual describes it as RTK positioning combined with VSLAM and visual-inertial odometry (VIO). It uses a triple-camera setup with a 300° field of view plus a ToF camera, and it supports dual RTK antennas. Where **Network RTK** coverage is available, the page says you don't need to install a local antenna, and network access and the cellular data it needs are included free. The page has a postcode coverage checker. A GNSS antenna is in the box for yards without coverage, and a second antenna for weak-signal areas is sold separately.

From the **Navimow X3 series user manual** linked from the US product page (Specifications table, X350 column; US/Canada "N" model):

- Recommended mowing area: **1.5 acres**
- Max incline **inside the working area: 50%**. Max incline **at the boundary: 25%**.
- Cutting width: **9.33 in**, 6 blades, anti-clogging disc. Cutting height (US/Canada): **2–4 in**, set electronically.
- Typical mowing time per full charge: **about 200 min**. Charging time: **about 80 min**. Area per hour: **about 0.087 acre**.
- Battery: **10 Ah / 216 Wh**, 21.6 V lithium-ion
- Net weight: **43.74 lbs** (battery included). Size: **27.5 × 21.7 × 12.1 in**.
- Noise: **60 dB(A)** sound power and **52 dB(A)** sound pressure
- **IP66** for the mower, charging station and power supply
- Drive: omni-directional front wheel and rear TPU tyres on geared hub motors (rear-wheel drive)
- Warranty in the manual: **3 years** for the X350N; battery packs and power adapters **2 years**

## Who it's for

Owners of about **one to one and a half acres** with front and back yards, narrow pathways, or areas under trees, which are the layouts the page names for EFLS 3.0. Setup is wire-free. Where Network RTK covers your postcode, you connect through the app with no local antenna. Elsewhere, you mount the included GNSS antenna where it can see the sky.

Check two things first. The **50%** rating applies inside the work area, and the manual caps the **boundary at 25%**. Also, the mower is **rear-wheel drive**. Skip it if the yard needs AWD traction, if the lawn is an acre or less (the X330 covers that for $500 less), or if it is well over 1.5 acres (the X390 is rated for 2.5).

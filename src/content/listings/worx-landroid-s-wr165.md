## What it is

Worx Landroid S WR165 is a **boundary-wire** robot mower for lawns up to **⅛ acre**. It is not the Vision Cloud line: no cameras, no RTK Cloud, no virtual map. A buried (or pegged) loop defines the work area. Worx’s AIA turning is for narrow passages *inside* that loop.

The US product identity on the official page is **Landroid S – 1/8 Acre** (page title “Landroid S 20V Robotic Lawn Mower (1/8 Acre)”, configurable option **WR165 - Landroid S - 1/8 Acre**, SEO slug, meta “Mow up to 1/8 acre”, catalog name, and main gallery asset named “Landroid S 1/8 Acre Main Image”). Official Display/Offer on 6 Sep 2026 was **$999.99** for this SKU; inventory was **Unavailable** (qty 0).

## Who it's for

Flat-to-moderate suburban lawns around **an eighth acre** where the owner will install perimeter wire (or pay someone to) and already owns, or wants, Worx 20V PowerShare tools. Official maximum slope on this SKU’s INFO_SPECS block is **30%**. Wrong machine if you will not bury wire, if the grass is over an eighth acre once you measure, if you wanted the quarter-acre WR147 or half-acre WR155, or if you wanted wire-free Vision Cloud WR310.

## Honest verdict

This is the wire-era Landroid sized for a measured **eighth-acre**: PowerShare 2.0 Ah battery, perimeter wire in the install, no RTK pole and no cameras. The catch is the install and the limits on the page — **30% slope**, **18 cm** of blade, **manual** height, and you have to bury that loop. If you want a quarter acre, that is WR147 (already listed); half acre is WR155 (already listed); wire-free vision is WR310 (already listed), not this SKU.

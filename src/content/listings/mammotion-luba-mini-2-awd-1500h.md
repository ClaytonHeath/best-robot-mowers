## What it is

The LUBA mini 2 AWD 1500H is the **High Version** of Mammotion's LUBA mini 2 AWD, rated for **0.37 acre**. It is a wire-free, four-wheel-drive robot mower that navigates with **360° LiDAR plus dual-camera AI vision**. Mammotion's product page headlines it "No Wire, No Antenna". This listing is locked to US variant **51222244819242**, SKU **MTLA25LUM215HHNA**, titled "LUBA mini 2 AWD 1500 | 0.37 acre / High Version 2.2"-4.0"". It is not the Standard Version (SKU MTLA25LUM215HNA, 0.8–2.6 in cut, $1,999 and sold out on 27 Sep 2026), not the first-generation LUBA mini AWD, and not the LUBA 3 AWD.

From the official US spec table (LUBA mini 2 AWD 1500/1500H column):

- Max lawn size: **0.37 acre**
- Positioning and navigation: **360° LiDAR + dual-camera AI vision**
- Max LiDAR range: **200 ft** (the table says "diametral"); LiDAR field of view **360° × 45°**
- Max climbing ability: **80% (38.6°)**
- Drive: **AWD (four-wheel drive)**
- Cutting width: **7.8 in main cutting disc** plus a **4.7 in edge cutting disc**
- Cutting height: **2.2–4.0 in on the H version** (the S version is 0.8–2.6 in)
- Cutting system: 6 pivoting razor blades + 3 pivoting razor blades; cutting-disc motor power **88 W**
- Battery: **6.1 Ah**
- Mowing area: **5,380 sq ft per charge**
- Charging time: **150 min**; mowing time per charge: **150 min**
- Multi-zone management: up to **20** zones
- Size (H version): **22.9 × 18.2 × 12.5 in**; net weight (H version): **39.5 lbs**
- Connectivity: **4G, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi**; a **4G module with 3 years of data** is included
- Waterproofing: **IPX6**
- Warranty: **3 years**

## Who it's for

This suits a front or back lawn of about a third of an acre with slopes, ruts, or uneven ground where a rear-wheel-drive mower would spin. The published rating is 80%, which is the same figure Mammotion gives the larger LUBA 3 AWD. It also suits you if you'd rather not plan around an RTK reference station. Navigation is LiDAR + cameras only. The box contains the mower, an extra set of blades, the charging station and its power supply, with **no RTK antenna and no boundary wire**, so the only thing to site is the charging dock. You map the lawn in the Mammotion app, either by driving the mower around the edge or with auto-mapping.

The High Version is for lawns you keep at **2.2 inches or taller**. If you cut shorter than that, this SKU cannot go there; you would need the Standard Version, which was sold out on this date. Skip it if the lawn is over **0.37 acre**, or if you want a wide deck that finishes big open areas quickly: the main disc is 7.8 inches.

## Standout details

- **Coverage:** 0.37 acre max lawn size. Up to 20 managed zones with paths between separate areas. Cloud backup holds up to 10 lawn maps, so one mower can be moved between properties.
- **Navigation and setup:** 360° LiDAR (200 ft range, 360° × 45° per the spec table) fused with dual-camera AI vision. No RTK station, antenna pole or perimeter wire. Partial remapping lets you redraw one edge without remapping the whole lawn. DropMow mows a spot once without saving a map. The page claims 300+ obstacle types are detected and says it works under trees and at night.
- **Drive and slope:** Four-wheel drive, 80% (38.6°) max climb.
- **Cutting:** 7.8-inch main disc plus a fixed 4.7-inch edge disc that, per the page, mows to about **2.1 inches from walls**. Height 2.2–4.0 inches on this H version, set in the app. The page copy says it mows 27-inch narrow paths.
- **Battery and runtime:** 6.1 Ah, 150 min mowing and 150 min charging per the spec table. Adaptive recharge (it charges only enough to finish the job), low-temperature battery preheating, and off-peak charging are listed.
- **Connected features:** Built-in 4G with 3 years of free service, app remote control, and live video streaming of the yard.
- **Build:** 39.5 lbs (H version), IPX6, rain detection.
- **Offer on this date:** The page shows a free Mower Cleaning Stand ($79 value) with this variant. It did not appear in the cart after adding the mower, so confirm it at checkout.

## Honest verdict

At **$1,699** ($1,999 compare-at, which is also the Standard Version's price), the 1500H gives a 0.37-acre hilly yard AWD, an 80% slope rating and LiDAR + vision mapping, with no antenna pole to site and no wire to bury. The directory's LUBA 3 AWD 1500 has the same 0.37-acre and 80% ratings, but it was **$2,399** (High Version) in the same store on the same day. For the extra $700 you get a 15.7-inch cut, a 9.4 Ah battery, 7,000 sq ft per charge and a 90-minute recharge. The mini 2 has a 7.8-inch main disc, 5,380 sq ft per charge and 150 minutes to recharge. Buy the mini 2 if the yard is small, sloped and kept at 2.2 inches or taller. Skip this High Version if you mow short, and skip the model if the lawn is over 0.37 acre.

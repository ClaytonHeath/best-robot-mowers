## What it is

The Worx Landroid Vision Cloud 4WD WR346 is the 1.5-acre top model in Worx's wire-free, four-wheel-drive Vision Cloud 4WD line on the official US store. This listing covers only the **1½ Acre / WR346** configuration: partNumber and SKU **WR346**, catalog entry id **3074457345616683944** (under parent product **3074457345616683236**).

It shares a configurable PDP with three smaller Vision Cloud 4WD siblings that are already listed on this site: [WR341](/mowers/worx-landroid-vision-cloud-4wd-wr341/) (¼ acre, $1,999.99), [WR342](/mowers/worx-landroid-vision-cloud-4wd-wr342/) (½ acre, $2,299.99), and [WR344](/mowers/worx-landroid-vision-cloud-4wd-wr344/) (1 acre, $2,999.99). It is also a different chassis from the two-wheel-drive Landroid Vision Cloud [WR310.1](/mowers/worx-landroid-vision-cloud-wr310/) (¼ acre, $1,199.99, 30% slope) and its 2WD siblings WR320.1 and WR340.

From the official US specifications block for partNumber WR346 and the WR344/WR346 owner's manual linked directly from that page (checked **29 Oct 2026**):

- Recommended lawn size: **1.5 acre** (US spec table); manual cutting area **1.5 acre (6000 m²)**
- Navigation: **RTK Cloud** with a **stereo** camera (Vision AI) and **V-SLAM**; no perimeter wire
- Maximum slope: **84% (40°)** on both the US spec table and the manual; the manual caps it at **15°** when the included Cut-to-Zero edge module is fitted
- Drive: **4-wheel drive** with a terrain-adaptive body and front steering

- Weight: **39.9 lbs** with battery on both the US spec table and top-level catalog attribute; manual machinery weight **41.9 lb (19 kg)**

- Also listed: rain sensor, FiatLux headlight module, Find My Landroid module, Cut-to-Zero module, multi-zone management, over-the-air and USB updates

## Who it's for

Property owners with **1 to 1.5 acres of mowable grass** that includes steep banks, rough transitions, or multiple zones where two-wheel-drive mowers lose traction and where the 1-acre [WR344](/mowers/worx-landroid-vision-cloud-4wd-wr344/) ($2,999.99) falls short on capacity and battery size. Like the rest of the Vision Cloud 4WD family, the WR346 uses satellite RTK with cloud corrections instead of a reference antenna pole in your yard, backed by stereo vision and V-SLAM for obstacle avoidance and tree cover, plus built-in 4G and the FiatLux headlight for night or low-light operation.

Check two site requirements before buying:

1. **RTK Cloud coverage:** Run the **"Is RTK available at my address?"** tool on the Worx product page before ordering, because Worx's cloud correction network has to reach your property.
2. **2.4 GHz Wi-Fi at the charging base:** While the WR346 includes built-in **4G** cellular, the manual's setup and troubleshooting steps still call for a usable **2.4 GHz Wi-Fi** signal at the charging station (or repositioning the station / adding a Wi-Fi booster). No boundary wire is buried, though the box includes a **19.7 ft (6 m) magnetic No-Go Line** and stakes for small hazard areas.

Skip it if your mowable area is 1 acre or less (save $700 with the [WR344](/mowers/worx-landroid-vision-cloud-4wd-wr344/)), if RTK Cloud does not cover your address, or if you want a much wider deck on a 1.5-acre lawn—such as the 15.7-inch AWD [Segway Navimow X350](/mowers/segway-navimow-x350/) ($2,799) or 14-inch AWD [Sunseeker X7 Plus Gen 2](/mowers/sunseeker-x7-plus-gen-2/) ($2,999).

## Standout details

- **What the extra $700 over the 1-acre WR344 ($2,999.99) buys:** Both the [WR344](/mowers/worx-landroid-vision-cloud-4wd-wr344/) and WR346 share the same 4WD chassis, **84% (40°)** slope rating, **8.7 in** 3-blade deck, **1.57–3.54 in** electric height range, built-in **4G**, **FiatLux** headlight module, and **Cut-to-Zero** edge module. Stepping up to the WR346 raises the rated coverage from 1 acre (`4000 m²`) to **1.5 acres (`6000 m²`)**, upgrades the battery from a **5 Ah (`WA3675`)** pack to an **8 Ah (`WA3678`)** pack (+60% capacity), and upgrades the charger from **190 W / 5.0 A (`WA3805`)** to **230 W / 7.0 A (`WA3797`)** so the larger 8 Ah pack still recharges in **65 minutes** (versus 60 minutes on the WR344's 5 Ah pack).
- **Slope and the Cut-to-Zero catch:** The US spec table and manual both rate the 4WD drive system for **84% (40°)** slopes, with a terrain-adaptive chassis and front steering. However, the manual's technical data carries the same footnote as WR341, WR342, and WR344: **if the mower is equipped with the Cut-to-Zero module, Worx does not recommend operating it on slopes greater than 15°**. The Cut-to-Zero module (**110 mm** cutting width; three manual height settings of **2.6, 3.0, and 3.4 in** / 65, 75, and 85 mm) comes in the box, so steep properties must choose between close border trimming and the full 84% slope rating.
- **In the box:** Both the US `What's Included` tab (`Worx Landroid Vision Cloud 4WD WR346 ×1`) and the WR346 column of `Manual-WR346.pdf` list: Worx Landroid Vision Cloud 4WD WR346 robotic mower ×1, charging tower ×1, charging base plate ×1, power supply / charger (**WA3797**) ×1, **20 V 8.0 Ah** PowerShare battery (**WA3678**) ×1, Cut-to-Zero module ×1, FiatLux headlight module ×1, spare blade kits ×8, hex key ×1, screws ×8, magnetic No-Go Line **19.7 ft (6 m)**, and pegs / stakes ×10.

## Honest verdict

At **$3,699.99**, the WR346 completes Worx's four-model Vision Cloud 4WD lineup as the **1.5-acre** flagship. Compared with the 1-acre [WR344](/mowers/worx-landroid-vision-cloud-4wd-wr344/) ($2,999.99), paying $700 more gets you a meaningful hardware upgrade—an **8 Ah** PowerShare battery (`WA3678`) instead of 5 Ah, paired with a **230 W / 7 A** charger (`WA3797`) that keeps recharge time to **65 minutes**—along with the 1.5-acre rating, **84% (40°)** 4WD slope capability, built-in **4G**, **FiatLux** headlight, and antenna-free **RTK Cloud + stereo vision + V-SLAM** navigation.

The catch is that the cutting deck does not grow with the price or battery: at **8.7 inches**, the WR346 has one of the narrowest cuts in the 1.5-acre class, and Worx's manual still warns against slopes above **15°** when the included Cut-to-Zero edge module is attached. At $3,699.99 it also costs more than wider-deck 1.25-to-1.5-acre AWD competitors like the 15.8-inch [Mammotion LUBA 3 AWD 5000](/mowers/mammotion-luba-3-awd-5000/) ($3,299, currently out of stock) and the 13-inch [Kress EyePilot 4x4 KR284](/mowers/kress-eyepilot-4x4-rtkn-kr284/) ($3,599.99, 1 acre). Buy the WR346 if RTK Cloud covers your address, you specifically want an antenna-free 4WD mower on the Worx 20 V PowerShare battery system for a sloped 1-to-1.5-acre yard, and you don't mind longer mowing hours from an 8.7-inch deck.

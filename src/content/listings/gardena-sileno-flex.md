## What it is

Gardena **SILENO flex** is the current US-market SILENO sold on the official US store, us.gardena.com, as Article ID **15301-41** (Shopify product **8008607400123**, variant **46393531269307**, SKU **15301-41.000.00**, GTIN **4066407021921**). On 15 Nov 2026 (AWST) the official guest cart accepted that line at **$599.00 USD** with no compare-at and no discount. The PDP schema.org Offer read **InStock**. The same store also lists **SILENO life**, which showed a sold-out state that day and is not this listing.

It is a **boundary-wire** robot mower, not a wire-free RTK or LiDAR model. The official US page says it is "built specifically for the US market" and "Engineered in Germany." Navigation on this page is a **guide wire system, adaptable to 1 or 2 wires**, with **up to 5 remote start points**. You still define the work area with the included **boundary wire**.

From the official US product page (15 Nov 2026):

- Rated coverage: **up to 0.2–0.3 acres (8,700–13,000 sq ft, depending on grass type)**. This listing uses **0.3 acre** as the published upper end.
- **AllCut** cutting with **two cutting discs**, one described for low cuts and one for high cuts, plus a **height-adjustment knob**. The page does not publish numeric cutting width or height.
- App control via the **GARDENA Bluetooth App** from **up to 33 feet**, with a Scheduling Assistant. Recommended app use is on a smartphone or tablet with **iOS 17 / Android 9** or higher.
- An on-mower control panel with push buttons.
- The page says it can mow in **all weather conditions**.
- Purchase terms advertised on the PDP: **fast free shipping** and **free returns for 30 days**.

The box list on the page includes the mower, charging station, boundary wire, hooks, two double guide wires, four connectors and five terminals.

The official US page does not publish slope, cutting width, cutting height range, weight, noise or an IP rating. Those fields are omitted here rather than taken from non-US SILENO pages.

## Who it's for

Owners of compact US lawns that actually fit the **0.2–0.3 acre** band and who are willing to install the included boundary and guide wires. It suits a buyer who wants a simple, lower-priced wired mower with Bluetooth scheduling and the dual AllCut discs, and who can stay within 33 feet of the machine for app changes.

It is the wrong listing if you want a wire-free map, a published slope rating before you buy, or a yard larger than **0.3 acre**. In that case look at the wire-free models already in this catalog, including the quarter-acre [Sunseeker S4](https://www.bestlawnrobots.com/mowers/sunseeker-s4/) ($999.99 on 15 Nov 2026).

## Honest verdict

At **$599** (15 Nov 2026), the SILENO flex is one of the cheaper official US-cart robot mowers you can actually add to a cart today, and it ships with the wire kit in the box. Pay it if the lawn is really in that 0.2–0.3 acre band and you do not mind a traditional wire install. If you want to skip burying wire, the listed [Sunseeker S4](https://www.bestlawnrobots.com/mowers/sunseeker-s4/) covers a similar compact yard without an antenna, at $999.99 on 15 Nov 2026. Skip this flex if you need a published slope figure, a wider cut, or a lawn over 0.3 acre.

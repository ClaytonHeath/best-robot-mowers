## What it is

The Kress EyePilot 4×4 RTKⁿ KR282 is a wire-free, four-wheel-drive robot mower sold on the official US store at kress.com/en-us. This listing covers the **½-acre KR282** only: WooCommerce product id **1298492**, SKU **KR282**. Kress names it the "EyePilot 4×4 RTKⁿ 1/2 acre robotic lawn with Zero Trim and integrated 4G and Premium Security".

It navigates with **RTKⁿ**: satellite corrections arrive over Wi-Fi or 4G from Kress's reference-station network, so there is no base station to install in the yard. Vision-based mapping and obstacle-aware navigation handle the rest. The drivetrain uses **four motors** with real-time torque control, an **articulated body** that keeps all four wheels on the ground, and **front-wheel steering**. The **ZeroTrim** cutting system is built to cut close to edges.

It is not the two-wheel-drive [EyePilot KR272](/mowers/kress-eyepilot-rtkn-kr272/), not the larger EyePilot 4×4 KR284 (1 acre) or KR286 (2.5 acres), and not the Mission RTKⁿ line (the listed [KR161](/mowers/kress-rtkn-kr161/) and KR172–KR174).

## Who it's for

- Lawns up to about **½ acre**. Kress gives the KR282 a 24-hour capacity of up to ½ acre per day, not a separate maximum lawn size.
- Steep, bumpy or uneven yards: an **84% (≈40°)** slope rating and **2.4 in** vertical obstacle clearance.
- Owners who don't want to bury wire or site an RTK base station, and who have Wi-Fi or 4G coverage for the RTKⁿ corrections.
- Yards where cleaner edges matter (**ZeroTrim**).

## Honest verdict

At **$2,999.99** (22 Oct 2026), the KR282 is the EyePilot for hills. It keeps the RTKⁿ-plus-vision navigation, ZeroTrim edges, **IPX6** and **4G** with no ongoing fee, and adds **four-wheel drive**, front-wheel steering and an **84%** slope rating.

The catch is what that terrain ability costs. The listed EyePilot KR272 is **$2,499.99** for the same half-acre-per-day capacity, with a wider **9.5 in** cut, **26.68 lb** weight and a **58 dB** rating, but only **40%** slopes and rear-wheel drive. The KR282 cuts **8.7 in**, weighs **41.45 lb** and is rated **62 dB**. Buy the KR282 when the half-acre yard has slopes beyond 40% or rough transitions; on a gentle lawn, the KR272 does the same job for less.

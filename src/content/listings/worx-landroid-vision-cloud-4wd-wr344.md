## What it is

The Worx Landroid Vision Cloud 4WD WR344 is a wire-free, four-wheel-drive robot mower sold on the official Worx US store. This listing covers only the **1 Acre / WR344** configuration: partNumber and SKU **WR344**, catalog entry id **3074457345616683941**.

It shares a configurable PDP with three Vision Cloud 4WD siblings: **WR341** (¼ acre), **WR342** (½ acre), and **WR346** (1½ acre). It is also a different machine from the two-wheel-drive Landroid Vision Cloud, which this site already lists as [worx-landroid-vision-cloud-wr310](/mowers/worx-landroid-vision-cloud-wr310/).

From the official US specifications block and the WR344/WR346 owner's manual linked from that page (checked **6 Oct 2026**):

- Recommended lawn size: **1 acre** (spec table); manual cutting area **1 acre (4000 m²)**
- Navigation: **RTK Cloud** with a **stereo** camera (Vision AI) and **V-SLAM**; no perimeter wire
- Maximum slope: **84% (40°)** on both the spec table and the manual
- Drive: **4-wheel drive** with a terrain-adaptive body and front steering
- Cutting width / diameter: **8.7 in** (22 cm), **3 blades**, self-leveling blade disc, **2400 rpm** no-load speed
- Cutting height: **1.57–3.54 in** (40–90 mm), electric adjustment, six steps in the manual
- Battery: Worx PowerShare **20 V, 5 Ah** Li-ion (WA3675); charge time **60 min** on the included **190 W** charger (WA3805)
- Connectivity: **Bluetooth**, **Wi-Fi** (2.4 GHz), **4G**
- Weight: **39.9 lbs** with battery (spec table); manual machinery weight **41.9 lb (19 kg)**
- Water protection: **IPX6** (manual); the spec table marks a hose-washable deck
- Also listed: rain sensor, FiatLux light module, Find My Landroid module, Cut-to-Zero module, multi-zone management, over-the-air and USB updates

`coverageAcres` is that **1 acre** recommended lawn size, not a daily rate.

## Who it's for

This fits a lawn whose **mowable area is within about 1 acre**, especially one with banks, steps between levels, or several zones, where a two-wheel-drive mower would struggle. Navigation is satellite RTK with corrections delivered from Worx's cloud rather than a reference antenna you mount in the yard. Stereo vision and V-SLAM help with mapping, obstacle recognition, and shaded patches. Worx's page says RTK Cloud service is free for life and 4G connectivity is free. It does not publish a time limit for the 4G.

Check coverage before you buy. The PDP has an **"Is RTK available at my address?"** check, because the RTK Cloud correction service has to reach your property. The charging station also needs a usable **2.4 GHz Wi-Fi** signal: the manual's troubleshooting table covers repositioning the station, moving the router, or adding a signal booster. There is no perimeter wire to bury. The box does include a **19.7 ft No-Go Line** and stakes, and the manual suggests a mapping accessory strip or another physical separation where your grass runs straight into a neighbor's lawn.

Skip it if the grass area is over an acre (Worx sells WR346 for 1½ acres), if RTK Cloud does not cover your address, or if you want a wide deck that finishes a big lawn in fewer passes.

## Standout details

- **Slope and traction:** 4WD, a terrain-adaptive body that keeps all four wheels grounded, front steering to reduce turf scuffing, and an **84% (40°)** published maximum slope. Manual footnote: **with the Cut-to-Zero module fitted, Worx does not recommend slopes greater than 15°**. That module is listed as included, so if your yard is steep, plan to remove it on the steep sections or accept the lower figure.

## Honest verdict

At **$2,999.99**, the WR344 is the 1-acre model in Worx's Vision Cloud 4WD line. You get **RTK Cloud** plus stereo vision with no perimeter wire and no yard reference station, **four-wheel drive** with a published **84%** slope, **4G** built in, and a **60-minute** charge on a PowerShare battery. That suits a hilly or broken-up lawn of up to an acre.

The hard limits are coverage, deck width, and the edge module. RTK Cloud has to reach your address, so run Worx's address check first. The **8.7-inch** cut is narrow for an acre. Worx's own manual also advises against slopes over **15°** with the included Cut-to-Zero module fitted, so the 84% figure applies with that module off. Buy the WR344 when the lawn is up to an acre, RTK Cloud covers your property, and traction on slopes matters more than cutting speed.

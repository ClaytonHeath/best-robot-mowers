## What it is

Anthbot **Genie 1000** is the **$799** wire-free SKU on the official US Genie PDP (Shopify variant **Genie 1000 | 0.49 acre**, SKU **105010025**, variant id **50551095558446**). It is cart-live on 9 Sep 2026 — distinct from the already-listed **Genie 600e (4G)** (`anthbot-genie`, SKU **105010062**, $699). Navigation is **full-band RTK + 3D vision (4 cameras)** with no perimeter wire. The same PDP also sells Genie 600, Genie 600e(4G), and Genie 3000; this listing locks the **Genie 1000** cart line only.

## Who it's for

Medium residential lawns near Anthbot’s **Recommended Mowing Area of 1000 m² (1/4 Acres)** ≈ **0.25 acre** where the owner will install the included RTK reference station and on-lawn charging station, wants app multi-zone mapping (20 zones) without burying wire, and wants **built-in 4G** (1 year free Connect+). Official **Maximum Slope Ability** is **24° / 45%**. Wrong machine if the measured yard is past this SKU’s marketed **0.49 acre / up to 2000 m²** ceiling, if you only need the smaller **Genie 600e** recommended ~600 m² / 0.22-acre-label yard, if you need the larger **Genie 3000** (0.89-acre variant label / recommended 3000 m²), or if you cannot place the RTK base with clear sky view.

## Standout details

- **Price:** Official US cart accepted Genie 1000 at **$799.00** (compare-at **$1,199.00**) on 9 Sep 2026; parent PDP `product:price:amount` meta is **699.00** USD (default 600e row) — do not use that meta as Genie 1000’s price.
- **Coverage:** Specs comparison **Recommended Mowing Area = 1000 m² (1/4 Acres)** ≈ **0.25 acre** — used for `coverageAcres`. The variant option and model-chooser chart on the same PDP still label this SKU **0.49 acre** / **up to 2000 m²**; treat that as the marketed ceiling, not the recommended yard-fit (same recommended-vs-marketed pattern as Genie 600e).
- **Navigation:** **Full-Band RTK + 3D Vision (4 Cameras)**; auto-mapping; multi-zone (**20** zones). Wire-free.
- **4G / anti-theft:** Specs list **1 year free** 4G / Connect+ (renewable annually at $39.90). **GPS Tracking**, **Out-of-Boundary Alarm**, and **Find My** are **Y** on Genie 1000. PDP marketing also calls out anti-theft digital fence as exclusive to Genie 1000 & Genie 3000.
- **Slope / terrain:** **24° / 45%** maximum slope; vertical passing **50 mm**; driving engine **2 Rear Wheels**.
- **Cut:** Width **20 cm (7.9 inch)**; height **3–7 cm (1.2–2.8 in)**, adjustment method **Auto**; **1 disc, 5 blades**; marketing copy also cites app-adjusted **3000 rpm** blade disc.
- **Battery (Genie 1000):** **5 Ah / 90 Wh**; charge ≈ **120 min**; mow time per charge **120 min**; **150 m²/h** (specs table).
- **Build:** Noise **≤ 58 dB**; waterproof **IPX6**; net weight **12.5 kg (27.1 lbs)** (PDP text writes “27.1bs”); size **63×41×27 cm**; Wi-Fi, cellular (4G), and Bluetooth; Device & Charging Station warranty **3 years**, Battery **2 years** (specs table).
- **In the box (PDP packaging list, shared Genie series):** Genie, blade set, charging station, adapter + extension cable, RTK mounting pole, RTK tripod fixing stake, RTK reference station, tool kit.

## Honest verdict

Genie 1000 is the mid live-cart Genie for a **recommended ~1000 m² / 1/4 acre** wire-free yard with RTK + quad cameras, built-in 4G, and a published **45%** slope limit. Buy it for that recommended footprint and the $799 official US price — not for a measured near-half-acre if you treat the **0.49-acre / 2000 m²** label as guaranteed single-pass capacity, and not if you actually need the smaller **600e** or larger **3000** SKU on the same PDP.

## What it is

Mammotion **LUBA 2 AWD 10000HX** is the **H Version** of LUBA 2 AWD sold on the official US store, locked to variant **49781963522346**, SKU **MTL24LUX3HUS02**, titled "LUBA 2 AWD 10000HX | 2.5 acres". On 27 Nov 2026 (AWST) the official US price was still **$4,499.00 USD** with no compare-at and no discount, as on 13 Nov, 17 Nov, 18 Nov, and 20 Nov 2026; a guest cart accepted that line even though product.js now reports available=false. The same PDP also sells **3000HX** (0.75 acre, $2,599) and **5000HX** (1.25 acres, $2,999), which a guest cart also accepted on 27 Nov 2026 at those prices while product.js reported both unavailable. 5000HX had also been accepted in the official guest cart on 20 Nov 2026, and had been back in that cart on 18 Nov 2026 after being sold out on 17 Nov 2026. Those siblings are not this listing.

It is a wire-free, four-wheel-drive robot mower. Navigation on this page is **UltraSense AI Vision** plus an **RTK reference station that ships in the box** (station, antenna, power supply, and a 10 m extension cable), so you do not bury perimeter wire, but you do have to site and power the RTK hardware. This is **LUBA 2**, not the listed [LUBA 3 AWD](https://www.bestlawnrobots.com/mowers/mammotion-luba-3-awd-5000/) family and not [LUBA mini 2 AWD 1500H](https://www.bestlawnrobots.com/mowers/mammotion-luba-mini-2-awd-1500h/). The page itself links "View the All-New LUBA 3 AWD".

From the official US product page (13 Nov 2026):

- Rated coverage for this variant: **2.5 acres**.
- All-wheel drive with a published **80% (38.6°)** slope rating and vertical obstacle clearance **up to 3.2 in**.
- **15.7 in** dual cutting discs; the buy box locks this H Version to cutting height **2.2–4.0 in**. (Elsewhere the same page also says "adjustable mowing heights from 1 in to 4 in"; this listing uses the H Version figure on the buy box.)
- **165W** cutting-disc motors.
- Up to **100** mowing zones.
- Built-in **4G** with **one year** of free 4G service; **3-year** warranty; free shipping and a **30-day** returns policy are advertised on the PDP.

The page also uses a series headline of "**1.25 acres/day** performance with a 15.7 in dual cutting disc" and a "**Maximum 15Ah** lithium battery" claim that is **not** broken out for the 10000HX SKU, so daily-rate and battery capacity are omitted from frontmatter.

The box list on the page includes the mower, charging station and its power supply, the RTK reference station, RTK antenna, RTK power supply, and a 10 m RTK extension cable.

## Who it's for

Yards that actually approach the **2.5-acre** rating, with slopes or uneven ground that need **AWD** toward the **80%** figure, and an owner willing to mount and power the included RTK station. The **2.2 in** minimum cut on this H Version suits lawns kept at that height or taller.

It is the wrong listing if a listed LUBA 3 already covers the lawn, if you wanted LiDAR instead of this LUBA 2's vision + RTK setup, or if a smaller sibling on the same PDP is enough — a guest cart still accepted 3000HX at $2,599 and the 1.25-acre 5000HX sibling at $2,999 on 27 Nov 2026, even though product.js reports those variants unavailable.

## Honest verdict

At **$4,499** (still the official US cart price on 27 Nov 2026, even though product.js now reports available=false), the 10000HX is the 2.5-acre LUBA 2 H Version, with AWD, an 80% slope claim, and a 15.7 in dual-disc cut. Pay it if the lawn is really in that size band and you will set up the included RTK station. A guest cart still accepted the 0.75-acre 3000HX sibling at $2,599 and the 1.25-acre 5000HX sibling at $2,999 on 27 Nov 2026. If a listed LUBA 3 covers the yard, start there — Mammotion is already selling LUBA 3 as the newer line on the same store. Skip this H Version if you mow shorter than **2.2 in**, and do not assume the page's **1.25 acres/day** headline means this 2.5-acre SKU finishes that area in a single day.

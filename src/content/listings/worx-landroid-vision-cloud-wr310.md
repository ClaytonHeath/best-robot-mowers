## What it is

Landroid Vision Cloud WR310.1 is Worx’s quarter-acre, boundary-wire-free robot mower. It pairs stereo Vision AI with **RTK Cloud** corrections (no on-site reference antenna) and V-SLAM for shaded or satellite-poor spots. Worx sells it as a drop-on, auto-mapping machine rather than a pole-and-wire install.

## Who it's for

Homeowners with about **0.25 acre** of grass, mixed hardscape edges, and a preference for no buried wire and no rooftop RTK mast. The 20 V 4 Ah battery is a Worx PowerShare pack, which matters if you already own Worx tools. It is a poor fit above 30% slopes or for lawns that are actually half an acre once you measure.

## Standout details

- **Coverage:** 0.25 acre recommended.
- **Navigation:** RTK Cloud + stereo camera + V-SLAM. Worx states lifetime-free RTK Cloud service. When satellites drop, the page says the mower fuses V-SLAM, inertial data, and odometry.
- **Slope:** Maximum slope **30% (17°)**. The owner's manual adds that with the Cut-to-Zero module fitted, Worx does not recommend slopes greater than 15°.
- **Cutting:** 8.7 in width, 1.57–3.54 in electric height, three blades, Cut-to-Zero module included on this SKU, self-leveling disc.
- **Battery:** 20 V 4 Ah Li-ion, 3 A charger, about 80 minutes to charge (WA3674).
- **Size:** 24.2 × 20.9 × 11.1 in; 30.9 lb with battery.
- **Other listed features:** Multi-zone management, rain sensor, hose-washable deck, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, over-the-air updates. Night vision (FiatLux) is optional on the line, not listed as standard on WR310.1.
- **In the box:** Mower, charging station, charger, 20 V battery, spare blades, a short “No-Go Line” (19.7 ft) and stakes — a physical assist, not a full boundary wire.

## Honest verdict

This is the “I do not want an antenna on the roof” small-lawn option, with a mature vision stack and a battery that may already be in your garage. The limits are printed in the spec table: a quarter acre, 30% slopes, 8.7 in of blade. Cameras still need light unless you add FiatLux, and cloud RTK still needs a network path. Measure the lawn before the price looks like a bargain.

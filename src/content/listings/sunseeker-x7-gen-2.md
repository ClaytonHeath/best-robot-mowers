## What it is

The Sunseeker X7 Gen 2 is a wire-free AWD robot mower sold by Sunseeker North America on its US store. This listing is the **0.75-acre** model only: Shopify variant **67587699769661**, product id **15396832313661**, a single Default Title variant. The store does not publish a SKU or barcode for it.

It is not the earlier X7 on the same store (`orion-x7-robotic-lawn-mower`, SKU ORIONX7), which was **$2,699.99** and **unavailable** on 4 Oct 2026. It is also not the X7 Plus Gen 2. The spec table on this page rates the Plus for **up to 1.5 acres** at **$3,299**, with the **4G-GPS anti-theft module included**. This Gen 2 cart is **$2,599**, and that module is **optional**.

From the official US spec table (4 Oct 2026), shared with the Plus except where the table splits them:

- Rated mowing area: **up to 0.75 acre** of mowable grass
- Navigation: **AONavi (RTK + VSLAM 2.0)**; vision: **Vision AI 2.0**, day and night; cameras: **binocular + iToF**
- Drive: **AWD + rear steering wheel**; max slope: **70% / 35°**; suspension row: **Front wheel**
- Cutting height: **0.8–4.0 in**, app-adjustable; cutting width: **14 in, dual disc**; blades: **6 × 2**; blade speed: **2,600 / 2,800 RPM**; cutting speed: **max 1 mph**
- Battery: **10 Ah** (no voltage is published)
- Waterproof: **IPX5**; noise: **60 dB(A)**
- Mowing zones: **no limit**
- Connectivity on this model: **Wi-Fi / Bluetooth** (the Plus row adds **4G**)
- 4G-GPS anti-theft module: **optional** on Gen 2, **included** on Plus
- Night mode with night vision, LED headlight, rain sensor, LCD, Alexa / Google Home, OTA updates, brush kit included

`coverageAcres` is that **0.75 acre** rated mowing area, not a daily rate. The FAQ says to measure grass, not the whole property, and that actual capacity depends on layout, conditions and schedule.

## Who it's for

This fits a lawn whose **mowable grass is within 0.75 acre**, with slopes, tree cover, or more than one mowing area, and where you want virtual boundaries instead of buried wire. Sunseeker pairs **RTK** with **VSLAM 2.0** so the mower can keep working when trees or buildings change satellite reception. Three independently driven wheels are rated for slopes **up to 70% (35°)**.

Plan the install before you buy. There is **no boundary wire**, but the FAQ says you still complete mapping and a positioning setup, and that you need suitable places for the **charging station** and **positioning equipment**. The published included list does not show a reference station (see conflicts below). The **4G-GPS** tracker is **not in this cart**. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are the connectivity row for this model.

Skip it if the grass area is closer to **1.5 acres** and you want 4G in the box: that is the X7 Plus Gen 2 at **$3,299** on the same page, not this variant. Skip it if you need a published weight or a separate boundary-slope number. Neither is on the spec table. No weight is published, so `weightLbs` is omitted.

## Honest verdict

At **$2,599** with **no compare-at**, the X7 Gen 2 is the smaller of the two new AWD models: **up to 0.75 acre** of mowable grass, **RTK + VSLAM**, a **14-inch** dual-disc cut, and a **70% (35°)** slope rating, with no boundary wire. What you still give up is the acre cap, the positioning setup, and 4G.

The FAQ still tells you to place **positioning equipment**, and the included list does not show a reference station. **4G-GPS anti-theft is optional** on this variant. The same page prices **X7 Plus Gen 2** at **$3,299** for **up to 1.5 acres** with that module included. Buy this Gen 2 if the grass area is inside **0.75 acre** and you do not need 4G in the box. The return window is **30 days and unopened**, unless the mower is faulty or incorrect.

## What it is

The Kress EyePilot RTKⁿ KR272 is a wire-free robot mower from Kress's EyePilot line, sold on the official US store at kress.com/en-us. This listing covers the **½-acre KR272** only: WooCommerce product id **1301127**, SKU **KR272**. Kress names it the "EyePilot RTKⁿ 1/2 acre robotic lawn mower with ZeroTrim and integrated 4G".

It navigates with **RTKⁿ**: satellite positioning with correction data sent over Wi-Fi or 4G from Kress's reference-station network, so there is no base station to install in the yard. **EyePilot vision-based awareness** handles obstacles, and Kress says a redundant positioning setup keeps it accurate when the satellite signal is weak. The **ZeroTrim** cutting system is built to cut close to edges.

It is not the EyePilot 4×4 models in the same US catalog (KR282 ½ acre, KR284 1 acre, KR286 2.5 acres), not the 2.5-acre EyePilot KR276, and not the Mission RTKⁿ line (the listed [KR161](/mowers/kress-rtkn-kr161/) and KR172–KR174).

## Who it's for

- Lawns up to about **½ acre**. Kress gives the KR272 a 24-hour capacity of up to ½ acre per day, not a separate maximum lawn size.
- Yards where cleaner edges matter and you would like to trim less (**ZeroTrim**).
- Owners who don't want to bury wire or site an RTK base station, and who have Wi-Fi or 4G coverage for the RTKⁿ corrections.
- Slopes up to **40%**. Steeper yards point to an AWD model.

## Honest verdict

At **$2,499.99** (21 Oct 2026), the KR272 is the entry EyePilot from Kress. It pairs network **RTKⁿ** with **vision-based obstacle awareness**, needs no perimeter wire or base station, cuts close to edges with **ZeroTrim**, and has a wider **9.5 in** deck, **IPX6** and **4G** with no ongoing fee.

The catch is value and terrain. Kress's listed KR161 covers a quarter acre for **$1,050.99** on sale (15 Oct 2026). The KR272 costs well over twice that for twice the daily capacity, and it adds the camera system and ZeroTrim. It is still **rear-wheel drive** with a **40%** slope rating, and the EyePilot 4×4 KR282 is the same-size step up for steeper ground. Buy the KR272 for a moderate half-acre where edge finish and obstacle awareness justify the price over a basic RTK mower.

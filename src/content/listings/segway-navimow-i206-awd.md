## What it is

The Navimow **i206 AWD** is the 0.15-acre model in Segway's US **i2 AWD** line. It is the smaller variant on the same official product page as the listed [Navimow i210 AWD](https://www.bestlawnrobots.com/mowers/segway-navimow-i210-awd/): variant **Navimow i206 AWD | 0.15 acre** (Shopify variant **45014242820233**, SKU **AA.12.07.01.0002**). On 2 Nov 2026 (AWST) the store listed it at **$999.00** with no compare-at, and a guest cart accepted it at that price in USD.

It is a perimeter-wire-free mower. It navigates with **EFLS™ NRTK** (Network RTK plus vision) and uses **VisionFence™** AI obstacle avoidance (150+ obstacle types on the US page). Drive is **Xero-Turn™ AWD**. Network RTK access and the cellular data it needs are included at no extra cost, and an antenna kit in the box covers areas without NRTK.

The NA user manual (§2.3, i206 AWD column) gives these figures:

- Recommended mowing area **about 0.15 acre**. The product page FAQ says the map can stretch to about **0.18 acre** depending on layout, with all zones sharing one map limit.
- Max incline **45%** inside the work area and **45%** at the boundary.
- Cutting width **7.1 in**, cutting height **2–3.6 in** (manual), 5-blade disc.
- Battery **2.55 Ah / 55 Wh** (21.6 V). About **60 min** mowing per full charge and about **60 min** charging. Area capacity **0.03 acre/h**.
- Noise **59 dB(A)** sound power (LWA), 51 dB(A) sound pressure. **IP66** mower and station, IP67 power adapter.
- Net weight **26.56 lb** with battery. Dimensions **21.4 × 15.1 × 11.2 in**.

## Who it's for

Owners of small lawns, about a seventh of an acre, where hills, roots or uneven ground make front-wheel drive slip, and who do not want to bury wire. It suits yards with good cellular coverage for Network RTK. The antenna is optional and is only needed where NRTK is unavailable.

It is the wrong buy if the lawn is bigger than 0.15 acre. The [i210 AWD](https://www.bestlawnrobots.com/mowers/segway-navimow-i210-awd/) on the same page covers about 0.25 acre for $1,299. On a flat small lawn, the FWD [Navimow i105](https://www.bestlawnrobots.com/mowers/segway-navimow-i105/) (0.125 acre, $799) is cheaper. If you want LiDAR instead of RTK, look at the [i215 LiDAR](https://www.bestlawnrobots.com/mowers/segway-navimow-i215-lidar/).

## Standout details

- **Price:** $999.00 in the official US guest cart on 2 Nov 2026 (AWST), no compare-at, no discount. It was $849 on sale on 9 Oct 2026.
- **Coverage:** **0.15 acre** recommended on the variant title, the manual and the FAQ. Map storage is up to about **1.2×** that area. The FAQ's **0.37 acre / 24 hours** daily figure, which it shares with the i210, assumes repeated recharges and is not the lawn rating.
- **Slope:** **45% (24°)** both inside the work area and at the boundary. The FAQ says this is up from 30% (17°) on the i1 series.
- **How it differs from the i210 AWD:** same body, cut, 45%/45% slopes, IP66 and 59 dB(A). The i206 has half the battery (2.55 Ah vs 5.1 Ah), so it mows about 60 min per charge instead of about 120 min. It is rated for 0.15 acre instead of 0.25, weighs 26.56 lb instead of 27.12 lb, and costs $300 less.
- **Connectivity:** built-in 4G, plus Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. The FAQ promises lifetime free NRTK and 1 year of free 4G data for the i2 series. The product page also offers Alexa and Google Home control.
- **Warranty:** the product page advertises a 3-year warranty and a 30-day return policy.

## Honest verdict

At **$999** (2 Nov 2026), the i206 AWD is the lowest-priced way into Navimow's AWD and NRTK + vision stack, and its 45% rating holds at the boundary too. Buy it if your lawn really is around 0.15 acre and has slopes or rough ground. The limits are the small rating and the 60-minute charge cycle. If you are near the 0.15-acre mark, the i210 AWD's bigger battery is worth the extra $300.

## What it is

The Segway Navimow H220 is the larger of the two **H2 series** robot mowers sold on Navimow's official US store at navimow.com. This listing covers the **H220** variant only: Shopify product **8367261089929**, variant **45014291415177** ("Navimow H220 | 0.5 acre"), SKU **AA.12.05.04.0003**. It is not the **H210** (0.25 acre, $1,799) on the same page, and it is not one of the older H-VF models (H800-VF / H1500-VF / H3000-VF), which navimow.com listed as sold out on this date.

Navimow calls the navigation **EFLS™ (LiDAR + NRTK + Vision)**. A built-in solid-state LiDAR, Network RTK, and a camera work together, so there is **no boundary wire and no RTK antenna** to install. Network RTK is included at no extra cost, and Navimow says the cellular data it needs is free.

From the official US product page and the **Navimow H2 US user manual** linked from it (Specifications table, checked **9 Oct 2026**):

- Recommended mowing area: **about 0.5 acre**. The FAQ says it may handle up to **0.6 acre** depending on layout, and maps can store up to 1.2× the recommended area.
- Max incline **inside the working area: 45%** (24°). Max incline **at the boundary: 25%**.
- Cutting width: **8.66 in**. Cutting height: **2–4 in**, with a **low-cutting kit (0.8–2.8 in)** in the box.
- Typical mowing time per full charge: **about 220 min**. Charging time: **about 120 min** (10–100%). Area per hour: **2,637 ft²**.
- Battery: **10 Ah / 216 Wh**, 21.6 V lithium-ion
- Net weight: **35.82 lbs** (battery included). Size: **24.6 × 17.5 × 11.6 in**.
- Noise: **60 dB(A)** sound power (LWA) and **52 dB(A)** sound pressure (LpA)
- IP rating: **IP66** for the mower and charging station, **IP67** for the power supply
- Warranty in the US manual: **3 years** on the H210/H220 and **2 years** on battery packs and power adapters

`coverageAcres` is the **0.5-acre** recommended mowing area. The page's "up to 0.93 acre per day" is a separate 24-hour throughput figure for the H220, measured on a flat lawn with no obstacles, and is not a lawn-size rating.

## Who it's for

The H220 fits a **lawn of up to about half an acre** that tends to trip up single-sensor mowers. Navimow pitches it for areas **under trees**, **narrow passages down to 2.3 ft**, **open terrain**, and **day or night**, with LiDAR, network RTK, and the camera covering each other's weak spots. The manual's setup is "Drop and Mow": place the charging station, then the mower maps the yard itself in the app. You don't bury wire or mount an antenna.

Check two things before buying. Network RTK depends on **cellular coverage**, and the page has a coverage checker. The included Connect+ 4G service is **free for 1 year, then $32.90 a year**. Also, the **45%** rating applies inside the work area. The manual caps the **boundary at 25%** and warns not to mow anything steeper than either figure.

Skip it if the lawn is well over half an acre, if the yard needs AWD and an 80%-class slope rating, or if a 0.25-acre yard would be served by the cheaper H210.

## Standout details

- **Navigation:** EFLS™ fuses a built-in **solid-state LiDAR** with **Network RTK** and **vision**. Navimow quotes about **200,000 points per second**, a **230 ft** detection range, and a **50 ms** scan interval, and says the LiDAR has no exposed rotating parts. The manual lists the LiDAR sensor, camera sensor, and RTK module separately on the mower.
- **Obstacles:** VisionFence™ recognizes **200+ object types**, detects objects down to **0.4 in**, judges clearance under overhangs, and slows down near animals.
- **Cut:** **8.66 in** width with a **100 W** mowing motor, **6 blades**, and an **anti-clogging cutting disc**. Height is **2–4 in**, or **0.8–2.8 in** with the included low-cutting kit. EdgeSense™ ride-on edge mode cuts as close as **2 in** from walls and fences. You can set up to **30 zones**, each with its own height and schedule.
- **Slopes:** Terrain Adapt plans routes on slopes, and an Electronic Stability Control system shifts the center of gravity in turns. The rating is **45% (24°)** inside the work area and **25%** at the boundary in the manual. The rear wheels are off-road wheels and the front uses caster wheels. The page doesn't call it AWD, so `driveType` is omitted.
- **Runtime:** **10 Ah / 216 Wh** pack, about **220 min** per charge, about **120 min** to recharge. Navimow claims **2×** faster charging and cutting than the previous generation.
- **Security and weather:** geo-fence alert, lift alarm, GPS tracking, and **Apple Find My**, with proximity unlock from your phone. It also has a rain sensor and forecast-based protection, plus **IP66** so the mower can be rinsed.
- **In the box:** H2 mower, charging station with installation kit, low-cutting kit, a **33 ft** extension cable for the power supply, the power supply adapter, 7 pegs, and 12 spare blades with bolts.

## Honest verdict

At **$2,199**, the H220 is the half-acre option for a tricky yard. It combines LiDAR, network RTK, and vision, with no wire and no antenna pole, and it is rated to mow under trees and through 2.3 ft gaps. A **10 Ah** pack gives about **220 minutes** per charge. It has an **8.66-inch** six-blade cut, **IP66**, a **60 dB(A)** rating, Apple Find My, and a 3-year mower warranty in the US manual. If the lawn is complex but only moderately sloped, that is a lot of navigation hardware for the money.

The limits are slope and drive. This is not an AWD machine. It is rated for **45%** inside the work area and only **25%** at the boundary. Network RTK and the 4G features rely on cellular service, and Connect+ costs **$32.90 a year** after the first year. The standard deck starts at **2 in**, and you fit the included low-cutting kit to go down to 0.8 in. Buy the H220 if the lawn is around half an acre and gives satellite-only or camera-only mowers trouble. Choose the H210 for a quarter acre, or an AWD machine if the slopes are steep.

## What it is

The Sunseeker X7 Plus Gen 2 is a wire-free, all-wheel-drive robot mower sold on Sunseeker's official US store, shopsunseekertech.com. It is the **1.5-acre** model in the X7 Gen 2 family. This listing is Shopify product **15396832444733**, variant **67587700293949**, a single Default Title variant. The store doesn't publish a SKU or barcode for it.

Navigation is **AONavi (RTK + VSLAM 2.0)**. RTK satellite positioning is combined with visual mapping, so Sunseeker says the mower keeps navigating as satellite reception changes around trees and buildings. Obstacle perception is **Vision AI 2.0**, which uses binocular cameras plus iToF depth sensing and works day and night. Three independently driven wheels give AWD traction. Front suspension handles uneven ground, and rear active steering is meant to reduce turf scuffing in turns.

**How it differs from the X7 Gen 2:** Sunseeker's own comparison shows the same navigation, drive and cutting system. The Plus raises the rated area from 0.75 to **1.5 acres**, includes the **4G-GPS anti-theft module** (optional on the X7 Gen 2), and adds 4G to Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

**In the box (from the US page's "What's Included" list):** the mower, a charging station, a charger for the charging station, 24 spare blades, 24 spare screws, 8 nails for the charging station, a cleaning brush, a quick start guide and an instruction manual.

## Who it's for

Owners of up to **1.5 acres of mowable grass** with slopes, trees or separate mowing areas. Sunseeker says to measure the grass itself and leave out buildings, driveways, pools and planting beds. The **70% (35°)** AWD rating covers most hilly lots, and the zone count is unlimited.

Plan the install first. There's no boundary wire, but the FAQ says you still have to complete mapping and a positioning setup, and place the charging station and positioning equipment where the setup guide says. The included-items list doesn't name a separate RTK station, so check that before you buy. Location tracking, geofencing and alerts depend on activation, network coverage and Sunseeker's service terms. Look elsewhere if your lawn is 0.75 acre or less, because the X7 Gen 2 covers that for less money.

## Standout details

- **Coverage:** up to **1.5 acres** of mowable grass, per the headline specs, spec table and FAQ.
- **Navigation:** RTK + VSLAM 2.0 (AONavi), Vision AI 2.0 with binocular cameras + iToF, night mode, and an LED headlight. Boundaries, mowing areas and no-go zones are set in the app, and the zone count is unlimited. Features include Efficient Cutting mode, spot cutting, multi-angle mowing and custom patterns.
- **Slope and drive:** **70% (35°)**, AWD + rear steering wheel, front-wheel suspension.
- **Cut:** **14 in** total width from two floating discs with six blades each, at 2,600 / 2,800 RPM. Height is **0.8–4.0 in**, set in the app. Maximum speed is 1 mph.
- **Battery, weather and noise:** 10 Ah battery, **IPX5**, **60 dB(A)**, rain sensor, cliff sensing.
- **Connectivity:** Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and 4G, with a 4G-GPS anti-theft module included. Also supports Alexa / Google Home, OTA updates, an LCD display and three return modes.
- **Support:** 2-year warranty and 30-day returns, per the store banner. The policy text limits returns to unopened mowers unless the unit is faulty.
- **Not published on the US page:** weight, run time per charge, and how long the 4G service lasts. They're left out rather than taken from other sources.

**Page notes:** The gallery images are shared with the X7 Gen 2, and the studio render shows an "X7 Gen2" badge on the body. The FAQ notes that some edges may still need trimming, depending on whether the lawn meets a flush hard surface.

Buy it if you have a hilly acre-plus lawn and want built-in anti-theft. Otherwise, save $700 with the X7 Gen 2.

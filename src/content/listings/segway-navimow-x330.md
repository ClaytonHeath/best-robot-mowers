## What it is

The Segway Navimow X330 is the one-acre model in Navimow's **X3 series**, sold on the official US store at navimow.com. This listing is Shopify product **7975880360073**, variant **43296343720073**, SKU **AA.12.03.05.0001**. It is not the X350 (1.5 acres) or the listed X390 (2.5 acres) on the same series page.

Navigation is **EFLS 3.0**. Navimow's US manual describes it as RTK positioning combined with VSLAM and visual-inertial odometry (VIO). It uses a triple-camera setup with a 300° field of view plus a ToF camera, and it supports dual RTK antennas. Where **Network RTK** coverage is available, the page says you don't need to install a local antenna, and network access and the cellular data it needs are included free. The page has a coverage checker. A GNSS antenna is in the box for yards without coverage, and a second antenna for weak-signal areas is sold separately.

From the **Navimow X3 series user manual** linked from the US product page (Specifications table, X330 column; US/Canada "N" model):

- Recommended mowing area: **1 acre**
- Max incline **inside the working area: 50%**. Max incline **at the boundary: 25%**.
- Cutting width: **9.33 in**, 6 blades, anti-clogging disc. Cutting height (US/Canada): **2–4 in**, set electronically.
- Typical mowing time per full charge: **about 160 min**. Charging time: **about 65 min**. Area per hour: **about 0.074 acre**.
- Battery: **8 Ah / 173 Wh**, 21.6 V lithium-ion
- Net weight: **43.74 lbs** (battery included). Size: **27.5 × 21.7 × 12.1 in**.
- Noise: **60 dB(A)** sound power and **52 dB(A)** sound pressure
- **IP66** for the mower, charging station and power supply
- Drive: omni-directional front wheel and rear TPU tyres on geared hub motors (rear-wheel drive)
- Warranty in the manual: **3 years** for the X330N

## Who it's for

Owners of up to **one acre** with front and back yards, narrow pathways, or areas under trees. The page names all of these for EFLS 3.0. Setup is wire-free. Where Network RTK covers your postcode, you connect through the app with no local antenna. Elsewhere, you mount the included GNSS antenna where it can see the sky.

Check two things first. The **50%** rating applies inside the work area, and the manual caps the **boundary at 25%**. Also, the mower is **rear-wheel drive**. Skip it if the yard needs AWD traction, or if the mowable area is well over one acre (the X350 is rated for 1.5 acres and the X390 for 2.5).

## What it is

Yarbo **Lawn Mower Pro** is the official US lawn-mower SKU built as a **modular Yarbo Core + Lawn Mower Pro module** (SKU **YAR-Y40-LM**), not a single sealed mower body. On 8 Sep 2026 the official US cart is live at **$5,499.00** (compare-at **$5,999.00**), **InStock**. The same PDP lists dual **300W** cutting motors, a **20 in (500 mm)** dual straight-blade deck, and app/auto height from **0.8–4.0 in (20–102 mm)**. Navigation is marketed as **PPVS / RTK + multi-sensor** with **binocular cameras**, ultrasonic sensors, and a bumper; official copy describes the setup as **wire-free**. What’s included on the PDP includes Yarbo Core, Lawn Mower Pro Module, docking station, Y Series battery, wired charger, Data Center, installation/tool kit, tow hitch, and straight blades.

## Who it's for

Owners with large residential or estate lawns near Yarbo’s **Max Mowing Size of 6 acres (25,000 m²)** who want a wire-free RTK + vision mower and accept a modular core-plus-deck system (and the included Data Center for local RTK, or NetRTK where coverage exists). Official **Max Climbing Ability** is **70% (35°)**. Wrong machine if the measured lawn is past that 6-acre ceiling, if you need a cheaper one-piece small-yard mower, or if you only want the multi-season **Lawn Mower Pro + Snow Blower + Blower** All-Season Pro bundle (different live SKU at $7,199 on its own PDP).

## Honest verdict

A serious large-yard modular mower with a live official US price and published 6-acre / 70% yard-fit numbers — buy it for Core + Lawn Mower Pro capability and RTK + vision navigation, not as a compact one-box robot. Hard limits remain the **6-acre** max mowing size, **70%** climb rating, and the **~$5.5k** modular system cost/weight.

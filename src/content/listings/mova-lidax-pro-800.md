## What it is

The MOVA LiDAX Pro 800 is the compact **0.2-acre** model in MOVA's US LiDAX lineup. MOVA's launch release puts it at 800 m², launched alongside the Ultra 2000 AWD. This listing is locked to US variant **47666572099820**, SKU **010901AA000250**, titled **MOVA Lidax Pro 800 Robotic Lawn Mower | 0.2 Acre**. The product has only that one variant. It is not the LiDAX Ultra 1000 (0.25 acre), not the Ultra 2000, and not an Ultra AWD model.

Navigation is **UltraView™ 2.0**. The official US spec table lists **360° 3D LiDAR + AI Vision** for positioning and obstacle avoidance, with remote-control + AI-assisted auto mapping. ## Who it's for

This fits a front or back lawn of roughly a fifth of an acre or less, with gentle slopes that stay within the published **45%**. It suits you if you want a LiDAR install you can power on and auto-map, without siting an RTK antenna. Dual maps and multi-zone control (up to **150** managed zones, per the PDP) cover a split front/back yard with one mower. The app also handles no-go zones and channels between zones.

Skip it if the lawn is bigger than **0.2 acre**, has slopes steeper than 45%, or has long borders you don't want to trim by hand. On MOVA's spec tables, the Pro 800 leaves grass within **4.3"** of the edge, while the Ultra 1000 leaves **under 2"**. Also skip it if you expect built-in 4G tracking: the Link Module is an optional accessory for this model.

- **Coverage:** 0.2 acre max mapping area on the spec table. Dual-map **Y**; multi-zone **Y**. When the battery runs low, the mower docks and resumes where it stopped.
- **Navigation / mapping:** UltraView™ 2.0 pairs 360° 3D LiDAR with AI vision. It needs no RTK station and no boundary wire. The PDP cites a **230 ft** LiDAR detection range, a **1080p** HDR RGB camera, and cm-level navigation. Night mowing **Y**.
- **Obstacles:** 3D point cloud + AI vision. Marketing claims **300+** obstacle types, including humans, pets, and small animals. Animal-friendly low-speed mode, animal activity zones, and do-not-disturb mode are listed.
- **Drive and slope:** Off-road rear-wheel drive; **45% (24°)** max climb; **≤1.6"** vertical obstacles.
- **Cutting:** **7.9"** width with a floating cutting system. Height is set in the app from **1.2"–3.9"**. Path planning is U-shaped. Modes: All-Area / Zone / Edge / Spot / Manual. Directions: Custom / Chequerboard / Crisscross. Pattern setting **Y**. You can set 2 mowing-efficiency levels (Standard / Efficient) and dual seasonal schedules.

- **Build:** **28.6 lbs**; **IPX6**; a rain sensor sends it back to the dock.

## Honest verdict

The Pro 800 at **$999** ($1,099 compare-at) fits a yard of up to **0.2 acre** with gentle slopes, if you want **LiDAR + vision** mapping with no wire or RTK hardware. On MOVA's own spec tables, it shares the Ultra 1000's dimensions, 7.9" cut, 45% RWD climb, 4 Ah battery and 60–80 min runtime. The Pro 800 is 1.6 lbs lighter, but it's rated for less area (0.2 vs 0.25 acre) and leaves a wider uncut edge (< 4.3" vs < 2"). On 26 Sep 2026 the Ultra 1000 was also **$100 cheaper** in MOVA's US cart ($899). Check both carts before buying, because MOVA's sale prices move often.

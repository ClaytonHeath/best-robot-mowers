## What it is

Anthbot **Genie 600e (4G)** is the **$699** wire-free SKU on the official US Genie PDP (Shopify variant **Genie 600e(4G) | 0.22 acre**, SKU **105010062**). It is a real, cart-live product on 7 Sep 2026 — not a vaporware landing page. Navigation is **full-band RTK + 3D vision (4 cameras)** with no perimeter wire. The same PDP also sells Genie 600, Genie 1000, and Genie 3000; this listing locks the **600e (4G)** cart line only.

## Who it's for

Small residential lawns near Anthbot’s **Recommended Mowing Area of 600 m² (1/7 acre, ≈0.15 acre)** where the owner will install the included RTK reference station and on-lawn charging station, and wants app mapping without burying wire. Official **Maximum Slope Ability** is **24° / 45%**. Wrong machine if the measured yard is past this SKU’s marketed **0.22 acre / up to 900 m²** ceiling, if you need the larger Genie 1000 (0.49-acre variant label) or Genie 3000 (0.89-acre variant label), or if you cannot place the RTK base with clear sky view.

## Honest verdict

Genie 600e is the entry live-cart Genie for a **recommended ~600 m²** wire-free yard with RTK + quad cameras and a published **45%** slope limit. Buy it for that small recommended footprint and the $699 official US price — not for a measured half-acre, and not if you ignore the gap between **recommended 600 m²** and the **0.22-acre / 900 m²** marketing label on the same option row.

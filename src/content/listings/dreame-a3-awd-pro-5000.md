## What it is

The Dreame A3 AWD Pro 5000 is the 1.20-acre SKU in Dreame Yard Care’s US A3 AWD Pro series. It is a perimeter-wire-free robot mower that maps with OmniSense™ 3.0 — official copy pairs **360° 3D LiDAR** with **binocular AI vision** — and does not use RTK or a boundary wire. Drive is all-wheel drive with hub motors (universal front wheels + off-road rear wheels).

Official US numbers published for this Pro series / SKU: working area capacity **5,000 m² / 1.20 acre** on the Pro 5000 variant, max slope **80% (38.7°)**, mowing width **40 cm / 15.8 in** dual-disc, cutting height **3–10 cm (1.2–3.9 in)**, EdgeMaster™ 2.0 disc-to-edge **< 1.2 in / < 3 cm**, weight **23.9 kg**, noise **≤ 65 dB**, waterproofing **IPX6**, and a **10 Ah** battery on the Pro 5000. Obstacle crossing is marketed at **2.2 in** (body copy also cites **2.16 in**). Dimensions in the spec table: **740 × 532 × 325 mm**.

It is not the non-Pro A3 AWD 1000/2000 (7.9 in cut, smaller capacities) and not the Pro 2500 (0.62 acre) or Pro 3500 (0.87 acre).

## Who it's for

Homeowners with up to about 1.20 acre who want a wire-free install that does not depend on an RTK antenna or Network RTK coverage, and who need AWD for slopes inside the published 80% work-area figure. App mapping is remote-control + AI auto-mapping; dual-map and multi-zone management are listed. Skip it if the lawn needs more than 1.20 acre of rated capacity, if you specifically want RTK/Network RTK, or if a smaller Pro 2500/3500 or non-Pro A3 AWD is enough.

- **Coverage:** Variant title and spec table — A3 AWD Pro 5000 = 5,000 m² / **1.20 acre**. Sibling Pro variants on the same PDP: 2500 = 0.62 acre; 3500 = 0.87 acre.
- **Navigation:** OmniSense™ 3.0; obstacle avoidance listed as **360° 3D LiDAR + binocular AI vision**. Marketing states “No RTK. No Wires.” Detection range footnote: up to 230 ft (70 m) under 80% reflectivity conditions.
- **Setup:** Wire-free. Boundary setup type: remote control + AI auto-mapping. Dual-map supported (separate charging station recommended per map when yards are disconnected).
- **Drive and slope:** AWD + hub motor; max slope performance **80% (38.7°)**; marketed obstacle crossing **2.2 in**.
- **Cutting:** **15.8 in (40 cm)** dual blades; height **1.2–3.9 in (3–10 cm)**; EdgeMaster™ 2.0 edge distance **< 1.2 in**. Modes listed: All-Area / Zone / Edge / Spot / Manual.
- **Battery:** Pro 5000 = **10 Ah** (Pro 2500 = 5 Ah; Pro 3500 = 7.5 Ah); charging current **5 A**.
- **Connectivity / security:** 4G Link service included **3 years** free for Pro 2500/3500/5000 (renewal purchase after). Lift-up alerts; AirTag compatibility noted (AirTag sold separately).
- **Other verified specs:** IPX6 rinseable; noise ≤ 65 dB (standard cutting mode footnote); weight 23.9 kg (**52.69 lb**); 3-year warranty stated on the US store page.

## Honest verdict

Buy the Pro 5000 cart if the yard is around or under **1.20 acre**, needs **AWD** toward the **80%** slope claim, and you want **LiDAR + vision** without RTK hardware plus the Pro series’ largest published battery. The locked official cart is **$3,499.99** (compare-at also $3,499.99 on the same variant — not a labeled MSRP). Skip it for larger-than-rated lawns, for RTK-first platforms, or for smaller lots better matched to Pro 2500/3500 or the non-Pro A3 AWD 1000/2000.

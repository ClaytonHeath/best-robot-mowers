## What it is

The Roborock RockNeo Q1 US page sells the **RockNeo Q110H** SKU — Roborock Garden’s entry wire-free robot mower in the RockNeo Q series. Official US copy pairs **Network RTK + VSLAM** (Amazon brand listing: **Full-band RTK + VSLAM**) with **Stereo Vision** obstacle avoidance under Sentisphere™ environmental perception. Setup is perimeter-wire-free; the boxed kit still includes an **RTK Reference Station** plus charging station, power supply, and blade set.

US yard-fit numbers published for this SKU / page: coverage **up to 1/4 acre per day** on the US PDP (footnote: internal EGMF-style 12-hour test including charging), Amazon title **Covers Up to 1/4 Acre**, and Roborock Help Center **Q110 up to 1,000 m²** (~0.25 acre). Max slope **45% (24°)** on the official DE Q110 specs table and on the US Amazon product title/bullets. Cutting width **22 cm / 8.7 in** (Amazon product details + DE specs). Waterproofing **IPX6** with rain sensing on the US PDP. Drive is **rear-wheel drive** (DE: Hinterradantrieb). PreciEdge™ edge module is **sold separately** (US: 1.2 in structural edge claim).

It is not the EU Q105 (500 m²), not RockNeo Q2 LiDAR, and not RockMow X115H/X120H/X130H AWD models.

## Who it's for

Homeowners with up to about **0.25 acre** who want a wire-free install with RTK + vision navigation, stereo-vision obstacle avoidance, and a published **45%** slope ceiling on rear-wheel drive. Multi-zone scheduling, wildlife-friendly quiet hours, PIN lockout, and optional 4G anti-theft module are listed. Skip it if the lawn needs more than ~1/4 acre of rated capacity, if you need AWD / higher slope (RockMow), or if you specifically want LiDAR without RTK hardware in the kit.

## Standout details

- **Coverage:** US PDP — up to **1/4 acre per day**; Help Center Q110 = **1,000 m²**; Amazon title — up to **1/4 acre**. Locked US SKU = **Q110H** (not Q105).
- **Navigation:** US PDP — **Network RTK + VSLAM** + Stereo Vision / Sentisphere™; Amazon — **Full-band RTK + VSLAM** + AI vision obstacle avoidance. Wire-free (no boundary wire).
- **Setup:** AI-powered mapping marketed on the US PDP; boxed **RTK Reference Station** included. Passages marketed at **2.3 ft** wide.
- **Drive and slope:** Rear-wheel drive; max slope **45% (24°)** (official DE Q110 specs + US Amazon title/bullets). Obstacle height **4 cm / ~1.57 in** on DE specs / Amazon bullet.
- **Cutting:** Width **22 cm / 8.7 in**, three blades (DE + Amazon). PreciEdge™ optional for closer edges (US: sold separately).
- **Other verified:** **IPX6**; rain sensing returns to dock; weight **12.3 kg / ~27.1 lb** (DE Q110); Amazon item weight **27 pounds**. Optional 4G + anti-theft module sold separately.

## Honest verdict

Buy the Q110H if the yard is around or under **0.25 acre**, slopes stay inside the published **45%** figure on **RWD**, and you want **RTK + vision** without burying wire. Official US list is **$1,299** (Amazon buy box was unavailable on check — not a live cart price). Skip it for larger rated lawns, AWD / steeper terrain, or LiDAR-first RockMow siblings.

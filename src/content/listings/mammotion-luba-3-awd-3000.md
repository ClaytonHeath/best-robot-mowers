## What it is

LUBA 3 AWD is Mammotion’s 2026 all-wheel-drive robot mower. Navigation is Tri-Fusion: 360° LiDAR, dual-camera AI vision, and NetRTK via Mammotion iNavi. The **3000** is the mid US coverage tier, rated for **0.75 acre (3,000 m²)** with **30-zone** management on the official model guide.

Unlike the 1500, this SKU is sold with **built-in 4G plus 3-year free 4G and iNavi Service™**, and the PDP states **lifetime free iNavi** so NetRTK can run without installing a physical RTK base station. The packing-list asterisk that excludes the RTK kit applies only to 1500/1500H.

## Who it's for

Yards that sit between small-lot and full-acre machines — roughly up to **0.75 acre**, with hills, trees, or multiple areas. Official AWD slope rating for the series is **80% (38.6°)**. It is the wrong listing if you only have ~0.37 acre (buy the 1500) or need **1.25 acres** (step to the 5000).

## Standout details

- **Coverage:** 0.75 acre on the 3000 / 3000H. Distinct from the 0.37-acre 1500 and 1.25-acre 5000 on the same US PDP.
- **Navigation:** Tri-Fusion — 360° × 59° LiDAR (up to 70 m range, 200,000 points/second on the series page), dual-camera AI vision, and NetRTK via iNavi (±0.4 in precision claimed for the Tri-Fusion system). No perimeter wire; no RTK antenna install required per PDP/FAQ.
- **Drive:** AWD; published series slope **80% (38.6°)**.
- **Cut:** 400 mm (**15.7 in**) dual cutting discs with two 165W motors. This listing locks **Standard** cutting height **1.0–2.7 in**; High (H) is **2.2–4.0 in** at the same cart price.
- **Zones:** Official model guide — **30-zone** management on the 3000 (15 on 1500; 50 on 5000).
- **Build:** **41 lb**, **IPX6**, noise **≤70 dB** on the US product page. Fits passages as narrow as **70 cm (27.6 in)** per series copy.
- **Connectivity:** Built-in 4G with three years included; Mammotion’s FAQ says a subscription is required after that. Live streaming is advertised for yard monitoring.

Sources: [LUBA 3 AWD US product page](https://us.mammotion.com/products/luba-3-awd-robot-lawn-mower), checked 21 Sep 2026 (AWST). Battery “up to 15 Ah / 215 minutes” is a series headline on that page and is **not** broken out per SKU there, so it is omitted here.

## Honest verdict

If your lawn is actually around **0.75 acre** and has slopes or awkward layout, the 3000 is the coherent mid LUBA 3: AWD, LiDAR, and NetRTK without wire. If you are under ~0.4 acre, the 1500 is the size match; if you need more than three-quarters of an acre, the hard limit is the **0.75-acre** rating — move to the 5000 or another acre-class machine. Budget for 4G after year three, and treat IPX6 as hose-washable, not submerged.

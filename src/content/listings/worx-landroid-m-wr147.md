## What it is

Worx Landroid M WR147 is a **boundary-wire** robot mower for lawns up to **¼ acre**. It is not the Vision Cloud line: no cameras, no RTK Cloud, no virtual map. A buried (or pegged) loop defines the work area. Worx’s AIA turning is for narrow passages *inside* that loop.

The US product identity on the official page is **Landroid M – 1/4 Acre** (page title, configurable option, SEO slug, and a gallery graphic labeled “1/4 ACRE”). Official Display/Offer on 5 Sep 2026 was **$1,199.99** for this SKU; inventory was **Unavailable** (qty 0).

## Who it's for

Flat-to-moderate suburban lawns around **a quarter acre** where the owner will install perimeter wire (or pay someone to) and already owns, or wants, Worx 20V PowerShare tools. Official maximum slope on this SKU’s spec block is **30%**. Wrong machine if you will not bury wire, if the grass is over a quarter acre once you measure, if you wanted the half-acre WR155, or if you wanted wire-free Vision Cloud WR310.

## Honest verdict

This is the wire-era Landroid sized for a measured **quarter-acre**: PowerShare battery, pegs and 820 ft of wire in the box, no RTK pole and no cameras. The catch is the install and the limits on the page — **30% slope**, **18 cm** of blade, **manual** height, and you have to bury that loop. If you want half an acre, that is WR155 (already listed); if you want wire-free vision, that is WR310 (already listed), not this SKU.

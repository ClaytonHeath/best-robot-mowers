## What it is

The MOVA LiDAX Ultra 3000 AWD is the **0.75-acre** SKU in MOVA’s US LiDAX Ultra AWD line. This listing is locked to the main US PDP variant **47355510325484**, SKU **010901AA000205**, titled **MOVA Lidax Ultra 3000 AWD Robotic Lawn Mower | 0.75 acre** (not the 【Live Only】 twin). It is not the 0.5-acre Ultra 2000 AWD, not the rear-wheel-drive LiDAX Ultra 1000 / Ultra 2000, and not a Dreame A3 AWD model.

Navigation is **UltraView™ 3.0**: official US copy pairs **360° 3D LiDAR** with **AI Dual Vision** for mapping with no boundary wire and no RTK base station (remote control + AI-assisted auto mapping, 2D and 3D map views). Drive is **AWD with four hub motors** on omni wheels. The official US spec table (Ultra 3000 AWD column) lists: max mapping area **0.75 acre** single map (**0.75 acre × 2** with dual maps), dimensions **740 × 532 × 321 mm**, weight **52.0 lbs**, cutting width **15.8"** (2 discs, 6 blades each, floating), cutting height **1.2"–3.9"**, electronic, UltraTrim™ 2.0 edge gap **< 1.2"**, max slope **80% (38.6°)**, vertical crossing **2.4"**, battery **36 V / 7.5 Ah / 243 Wh**, charging **5 A**, **65 min (15%–95%)**, runtime **150–170 min** per charge, **0.2–0.25 acre** mowed per charge, waterproofing **IPX6**, 4G service **3 years free**, and warranty **3 years**.

## Who it's for

Consider this cart if the mowable area is between roughly half an acre and **0.75 acre**, parts of it are steep or uneven, and you want a wire-free LiDAR install with no RTK antenna to site. The published **80%** slope figure, **2.4"** vertical crossing and four-wheel hub-motor drive are what set the AWD line apart from the rear-wheel-drive Ultra 1000 / Ultra 2000 (45%). Dual maps (up to **0.75 acre** each, with one or two charging stations) and up to **150** zones suit split front/back yards or two separate lawns.

Skip it if the lawn is **0.5 acre** or smaller: on MOVA’s own comparison table the Ultra 2000 AWD has the same published weight, deck, drive, battery, runtime and slope figures, with a 0.5-acre mapping area. Also skip it if you need more than **0.75 acre** on a single map, or if a gentle yard doesn’t need AWD, where a RWD Ultra covers it for less.

## Honest verdict

If the yard runs up to **0.75 acre**, includes slopes a RWD mower can’t hold, and you want **LiDAR + vision** with no wire or RTK hardware, the Ultra 3000 AWD at **$2,699** ($2,999 compare-at) fits that yard. Plan for several dock-and-resume cycles per full pass at **0.2–0.25 acre per charge**, expect a **52 lb** machine to move, and confirm the lawn really exceeds **0.5 acre** before paying the extra over the Ultra 2000 AWD. MOVA publishes identical hardware specs for the two, and the mapping area is the only published difference.

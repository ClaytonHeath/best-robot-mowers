## What it is

The GOAT A2000 LiDAR PRO is ECOVACS' mid-size LiDAR mower in the US A-series. It maps the lawn with the same **HoloScope 360 Dual-LiDAR** system as the larger A3000: a 360° rotating LiDAR plus a 3D time-of-flight LiDAR, with an AI camera (AIVI 3D) for obstacle recognition. A **TruEdge string trimmer** is built in for edges. The US instruction manual lists the model number as **MR2507C** and the working capacity as **2,000 m² (1/2 acre)**. At 1 acre = 4,046.86 m², that is 0.494 acre, and this listing uses ECOVACS' 1/2-acre figure. Store SKU is **GA2000LP** (UPC 850077202339).

**Setup:** No perimeter wire, RTK antenna, or RTK reference station in the box. Mapping is automatic, or you drive the mower around the edge with the app's remote control. The charging station needs strong 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi coverage and a flat, open spot, according to the shared A2000/A3000 US manual.

**In the box (US product-page Inbox / spec table):** the mower with 6 blades and screws pre-installed, a lens cover, and 3 m of trimmer line; a trimmer module, trimmer screws, and trimmer brush (2 pcs) to install; charging station with a 10 m power cable, charging-station pile, 8 fastening nails, power adapter, and Allen key; spare blade kit (12 blades + 12 screws) and a spare 3 m trimmer line; quick start guide and manual.

This is the smaller sibling of the listed [ecovacs-goat-a3000-lidar-pro](https://www.ecovacs.com/us/shop/goat-robotic-lawn-mower/goat-a3000-lidar-pro), not a renamed twin. Both share the 32 V dual-disc platform, TruEdge, HoloScope LiDAR, 50% / 20% slope pair, and IPX6 body. The hard differences are **coverage** (A2000 **1/2 acre / 2,000 m²** vs A3000 **3/4 acre / 3,000 m²**), **battery** (**3,000 mAh / 3.0 Ah** and about **50 min** mowing per charge vs **7,500 mAh / 7.5 Ah** and **160 min**), **charge time** (**50±5 min** vs **70±5 min**), **weight** (**39.24 lb** vs **40.34 lb**), **model number** (**MR2507C** vs **MR2507G**), and **price** ($1,452 promo / $1,999.99 origin on 30 Sep 2026 vs the A3000's $2,499.99 list in that listing). Cutting width is the same dual-disc setup: the A2000 page spec table lists **12.96 in**; the shared manual lists **33 cm (13.0")** for both.

## Who it's for

Owners of suburban lawns up to about half an acre who want edges trimmed without a handheld string trimmer and do not want to mount an RTK antenna. LiDAR mapping does not depend on a clear view of the sky, so it suits lawns shaded by trees or buildings. Look elsewhere if the lawn is larger than 1/2 acre (step up to the [ecovacs-goat-a3000-lidar-pro](https://www.ecovacs.com/us/shop/goat-robotic-lawn-mower/goat-a3000-lidar-pro) at 3/4 acre), if the mowing area includes slopes above 50%, or if your virtual boundary runs along a bank steeper than 20%. ECOVACS does not publish a drive type (such as AWD or RWD) for this SKU.

## Standout details

- **Coverage:** 2,000 m² (1/2 acre), from the US instruction manual. The product page's spec table does not list a working area. Page copy calls it suitable for "large lawns."
- **Navigation:** The spec table lists navigation as **Multiline LiDAR** and obstacle avoidance as **3D ToF + AI Camera + Multiline LiDAR**. ECOVACS claims 2 cm mapping precision, operation in the dark, avoidance of 200+ obstacle types (including hedgehogs), and stable avoidance at 1.97 in from an obstacle.
- **Slope and terrain:** Maximum work-area slope **50% (27°)**. Maximum virtual-boundary slope **20%**. Barrier crossing is given as 4 cm (1.2 in in Key Features). Narrow passages must be **2.95 ft / 0.9 m** wide. ECOVACS does not publish a drive type for this SKU.
- **Cut:** **12.96 in** cutting width on the page spec table (manual: **33 cm / 13.0"**), dual blade discs at **3,000 rpm**. Electric height **1.18–3.54 in**; the spec table shows a **(0.28)** step, while Key Features copy says **0.39 in** increments. Mowing efficiency is about 2,691–4,306 sq ft/h (page), with marketing also citing up to 400 m²/h.
- **TruEdge trimmer:** 7.09 in trimming width, 8,500 rpm head, and 118.11 in of trimmer line. The spec table rates blade edge mowing at 1.97 in, blade cross-boundary mowing at 0 in, and trimmer mowing at "~0 in".
- **Power:** 32 V energetic platform (page Energy Platform; battery section of the manual rates the pack at **32.4 V** / **3,000 mAh**). **50 min** mowing per charge and **50 min** charging time on the page spec table (manual charging time **50±5 min**). Output of the A2000 supply unit is 3 A.
- **Build:** **39.24 lb** mower, **IPX6** mower and **IPX4** station, plus lift, collision, and rain sensors. Body noise is **62 dB(A)** and trimmer noise **82 dB(A)** (sound power; the manual gives sound pressure as 54 and 74 dB(A)). Dimensions excluding the trimmer brush are **2.23 × 1.77 × 1.10 ft** on the page (manual: 680 × 540 × 336 mm / 26.8 × 21.3 × 13.2 in).
- **Connectivity:** Bluetooth and 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Cellular / GNSS appear in the manual as **purchased** options; the US store sells a GOAT Cellular Module Pro separately for A-series models.

**Page / manual conflicts:** Cutting width is **12.96 in** on the page spec table and **13.0"** in the manual — this listing uses the page figure in frontmatter and notes both. Cutting height is **1.18–3.54 in** on the page vs **3–9 cm (1.2–3.6 in)** in the manual; the page step size is listed as **(0.28)** in the table and **0.39 in** in Key Features. The manual's Basic Information block also shows a **37.8 V** rated voltage for the platform while the battery section and page Energy Platform use **32.4 V / 32 V** — this listing follows the page Energy Platform and the battery section. Spec table values win over marketing copy where they conflict.

## Honest verdict

For a half-acre lawn that needs edges trimmed, this is the A-series LiDAR package without stepping up to the A3000's price or battery. It maps with dual LiDAR, needs no RTK station or wire, is rated IPX6, and trims edges automatically. The published limits are coverage (1/2 acre), a 20% virtual-boundary slope, a ~50-minute runtime on the 3.0 Ah pack, and a trimmer that runs at 82 dB(A). Against the listed [ecovacs-goat-a3000-lidar-pro](https://www.ecovacs.com/us/shop/goat-robotic-lawn-mower/goat-a3000-lidar-pro) you give up 1/4 acre of rating and most of the battery (3.0 Ah / ~50 min vs 7.5 Ah / 160 min) for a cart price of $1,452 on 30 Sep 2026 versus that listing's $2,499.99. Against the smaller listed [ecovacs-goat-o1000-lidar-pro](https://www.ecovacs.com/us/shop/goat-robotic-lawn-mower/goat-o1000-lidar-pro) ($1,499.99, 1/4 acre, 18 V, 8.66 in cut) this is the wider dual-disc 32 V machine with twice the rated area. If the lawn is closer to three-quarters of an acre, the A3000 is the matching sibling; if it is a quarter acre, the O1000 is the closer fit on coverage.

## What it is

Husqvarna Automower 440 iQ is a residential, EPOS satellite-mapped robot mower. The US/CA SKU is **970 72 76-01**. Husqvarna leads with **2 acre lawn size (±20%)** — the same figure as **area capacity — systematic**. **Area capacity — irregular** is **1 acre**. Boundary type is **virtual wire or physical wire**. A normal install does not require buried loop wire.

The box includes the mower, charging station, 6 extra blades, 33 ft low-voltage cable, power supply, 100 stakes, EPOS Plug-in, and EPOS Reference station. **Loop wire is not included.** US dealer carts were **$4,299.99** on 30 Aug 2026. husqvarna.com has no visible cart.

## Who it's for

Large yards that will use EPOS virtual mapping — stay-out zones, transport paths, and systematic patterns from the Automower Connect app — and that actually measure at or under **2 acres** when mowed systematically. If the lawn is irregular, Husqvarna’s own capacity figure is **1 acre**. Official slope is **45% inside the work area** and **15% at the boundary**. It is the wrong machine if the grass is over about two acres, if the layout is a messy acre-plus, or if you need all-wheel drive (that is a different SKU).

## Honest verdict

This is the large-yard iQ Automower for a **systematic ~2-acre** lawn: EPOS virtual mapping, 9.4 in deck, IPX5, no buried wire required. The catch is capacity and terrain — Husqvarna itself rates **irregular layouts at 1 acre**, in-lawn slope at **45%** (15% at the boundary), and the install still includes an **EPOS reference station**. It is not the 435X AWD or 435 iQ AWD.

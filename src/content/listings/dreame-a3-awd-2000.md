## What it is

The Dreame A3 AWD 2000 is the 0.50-acre SKU in Dreame Yard Care’s US non-Pro A3 AWD series. It is a perimeter-wire-free robot mower that maps with OmniSense™ 3.0 — official copy pairs **360° 3D LiDAR** with **binocular AI vision** — and does not use RTK or a boundary wire. Drive is all-wheel drive with hub motors (universal wheels × 2 + off-road wheels × 2).

Official US numbers published for this series / SKU: working area capacity **2,000 m² / 0.50 acre** on the A3 AWD 2000 variant, max slope **80% (38.7°)**, mowing width **7.9 in**, cutting height **1.2–3.9 in (3–10 cm)** in 0.2 in increments, EdgeMaster™ disc-to-edge **< 1.91 in** (marketing also cites **< 1.9 in**), weight **16.1 kg (35.49 lb)**, noise **< 62.8 dB**, waterproofing **IPX6**, and a **5 Ah** battery. Obstacle crossing is marketed at **2.2 in** (body copy also cites **2.17 in**). Dimensions in the spec table: **635 × 434 × 295 mm**.

It is not the Pro series (15.8 in dual-disc; Pro 2500 / 3500 / 5000) and not the A3 AWD 1000 (0.25 acre on the same PDP).

## Who it's for

Homeowners with up to about **0.50 acre** who want a wire-free install that does not depend on an RTK antenna, and who need AWD for slopes inside the published **80%** work-area figure. App mapping is remote-control + AI auto-mapping; dual-map and up to **150** zones are listed. Skip it if the lawn needs more than 0.50 acre of rated capacity (look at the A3 AWD Pro line), if you specifically want RTK/Network RTK, or if a quarter-acre A3 AWD 1000 is enough.

## Standout details

- **Coverage:** Variant title and spec table — A3 AWD 2000 = 2,000 m² / **0.50 acre**. Sibling on the same PDP: A3 AWD 1000 = 1,000 m² / 0.25 acre. Marketing also lists power modes as Standard **0.25**, Efficient **0.50**, and Rush **0.54 acre/24 hrs** (throughput rates, not a higher capacity rating).
- **Navigation:** OmniSense™ 3.0; obstacle avoidance listed as **360° 3D LiDAR + binocular AI vision**. Marketing states “No RTK. No Wires.” LiDAR detection range footnote: up to **164 in (50 m)**.
- **Setup:** Wire-free. Boundary setup type: remote control + AI auto-mapping. Dual-map supported (separate charging station recommended per map when yards are disconnected).
- **Drive and slope:** AWD + hub motor; max slope performance **80% (38.7°)**; marketed obstacle crossing **2.2 in**; narrow-passage agility marketed at **≤ 23.6 in**.
- **Cutting:** **7.9 in** mowing width; height **1.2–3.9 in (3–10 cm)**; EdgeMaster™ edge distance **< 1.9 in**. Modes listed: All-Area / Zone / Edge / Spot / Manual.
- **Connectivity / security:** PDP marketing lists **1 year** of complimentary 4G data; lift-up alert, PIN lockout, and AirTag compatibility are marketed (AirTag sold separately).
- **Other verified specs:** IPX6 rinseable; noise < 62.8 dB; weight 16.1 kg (**35.49 lb**); 3-year warranty stated on the US store page.

## Honest verdict

Buy the A3 AWD 2000 cart if the yard is around or under **0.50 acre**, needs **AWD** toward the **80%** slope claim, and you want **LiDAR + vision** without RTK hardware on a **7.9 in** deck. The locked official sale is **$1,899.99** ($2,199.99 compare-at on the same variant — not a labeled MSRP). Skip it for larger rated lawns (A3 AWD Pro line), for a wider dual-disc cut, or for smaller lots better matched to the A3 AWD 1000.

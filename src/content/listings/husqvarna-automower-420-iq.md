## What it is

The Husqvarna Automower 420 iQ is a residential, EPOS satellite-mapped robot mower from Husqvarna's iQ series. The US/CA SKU is **970 72 75-01**. Husqvarna leads with a **1 acre lawn size (±20%)**, the same figure as its **area capacity — systematic**. **Area capacity — irregular** is **0.5 acre**. The boundary type is **physical wire or virtual wire**, so a normal install doesn't need buried loop wire.

EPOS can run through **Husqvarna Cloud** in select locations (the page has an address coverage checker). Otherwise it uses the **EPOS reference station included in the box**.

The box includes the mower, charging station, 6 extra blades, 33 ft low-voltage cable, power supply, 100 stakes, EPOS Plug-in, and EPOS reference station. **Loop wire is not included.**

## Who it's for

Suburban lawns of around **one acre** mowed systematically, or **about half an acre if the layout is irregular**, that will use EPOS virtual mapping instead of buried wire. Husqvarna's official slope figures are **45% inside the work area** and **15% at the boundary**.

It is the wrong machine if:
- the grass is well over an acre;
- the layout is a messy half-acre-plus;
- the edges of the lawn are steep;
- you need all-wheel drive, which is a different SKU.

## Honest verdict

This is the one-acre iQ Automower for a **systematic lawn of about an acre**. It has EPOS virtual mapping, a 9.4 in deck, IPX5 and no buried wire.

The 420 iQ shares the 410 iQ's chassis, deck, 5 Ah pack and slope ratings. It doubles the rated area and recharges in half the time (4.2 A vs 2.2 A charge current). The 440 iQ adds a larger pack and twice the area again. The two 435 models trade the 1–4 in deck for AWD and a 70% slope rating.

The limits are the halved irregular-layout rating, the 15% boundary slope, a typical 84-minute run per charge on a full acre, and stock. husqvarna.com showed it out of stock on 20 Oct 2026, so the purchase today is through dealers. Buy the 420 iQ for an open, moderately sloped lawn of about an acre. Choose an AWD model if the edges are steep.

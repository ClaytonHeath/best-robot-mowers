## What it is

The MOVA LiDAX Ultra 2000 is the **0.5-acre** SKU in MOVA’s US LiDAX Ultra (non-AWD) pair. This listing is locked to US variant **47255944298732**, SKU **010901AA000181**, barcode **850071220032**, titled **MOVA LIDAX Ultra 2000 Lawn Mower | 0.5 acre**. It is not the 0.25-acre Ultra 1000, not LiDAX Ultra 2000 AWD, not Ultra 3000 AWD, and not a Dreame A3 AWD model.

Navigation is **UltraView™ 2.0**: official US copy pairs **360° 3D LiDAR** with **AI vision** for RTK-free, perimeter-wire-free mapping (remote control + AI auto-mapping). Drive is **rear-wheel drive** with off-road wheels. Official US comparison table for this SKU: max mapping area **0.5 acre**, dimensions **26.2 × 17.5 × 10.7"**, weight **30.4 lbs**, cutting width **7.9"**, cutting height **1.2"–3.9"**, UltraTrim 1.0 edge distance **< 2"**, max climbing **45% (24°)**, vertical passing **≤1.6"**, battery **5 Ah**, charge **65 min**, mowing time per charge **70–90 min**, waterproofing **IPX6**, TrueGuard™ listed **Y**, and Link Module Service **Y (1-Year Free Usage)**.

## Who it's for

Buy this cart if the mowable area is around or under **0.5 acre**, you want a wire-free LiDAR install without an RTK station, and slopes stay inside the published **45%** work-area figure on RWD. Dual-map and multi-zone management (marketing: up to **150** zones) cover split front/back layouts when you accept docking and resume cycles on the **5 Ah** pack.

Skip it if you need only **0.25 acre** (cheaper Ultra 1000 on the same store), **AWD** traction or the wider dual-disc Ultra AWD line (**Ultra 2000 AWD** / **Ultra 3000 AWD**), **0.75 acre** rated capacity (Ultra 3000 AWD), or if you expected Dreame Yard Care branding — that is a different brand and catalog.

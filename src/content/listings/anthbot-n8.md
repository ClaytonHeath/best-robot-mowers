## What it is

Anthbot **N8** is the **$1,699** wire-free **N-series** SKU on the official US N8 PDP (Shopify **Default Title**, SKU **105010094**, variant id **51528020459822**). Cart/add.js accepted this line at **$1,699.00** on 14 Sep 2026. Navigation is **Full-Band RTK + NRTK + Dual Vision** (150° HDR AI vision) with **4G**, no perimeter wire, and an optional physical RTK station path marketed on the same page. Working modes are **Mow, Mulch, Collect, Sweep** with a published **23L** auto-dumping bag. This is the **N8** SKU — **not** listed **Anthbot Genie 600e (4G)** (`anthbot-genie`), **Genie 1000** (`anthbot-genie-1000`), **Genie 3000** (`anthbot-genie-3000`), **M5** (`anthbot-m5`), **M5 LiDAR** (`anthbot-m5-lidar`), or **M9** (`anthbot-m9`). This PDP is single-variant; this listing locks that base cart line only.

## Who it's for

Suburban lawns near Anthbot’s published **Recommended Mowing Area of 1,500㎡ (3/8 Acres)** ≈ **0.37 acre** (PDP intro / feature strip also say designed for lawns **up to 1500 m²**) where the owner wants wire-free RTK setup, dual vision when satellites are obstructed, multi-zone app control, and clipping/leaf **collect + auto dump** (or mulch/sweep modes) rather than cut-only. Official **Maximum Slope Ability** is **24° / 45%**. Wrong machine if the measured yard is past **1,500 m²**, if you only need a smaller listed **M5 / M5 LiDAR / M9** footprint, or if you actually need a listed **Genie** acreage SKU.

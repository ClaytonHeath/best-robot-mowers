## What it is

Anthbot **Genie 3000** is the **$899** wire-free SKU on the official US Genie PDP (Shopify variant **Genie 3000 | 0.89 acre**, SKU **105010042**, variant id **49940022722862**). It is cart-live on 10 Sep 2026 — distinct from the already-listed **Genie 600e (4G)** (`anthbot-genie`, SKU **105010062**, $699) and **Genie 1000** (`anthbot-genie-1000`, SKU **105010025**, $799). Navigation is **full-band RTK + 3D vision (4 cameras)** with no perimeter wire. The same PDP also sells Genie 600, Genie 600e(4G), and Genie 1000; this listing locks the **Genie 3000** cart line only.

## Who it's for

Larger residential lawns near Anthbot’s **Recommended Mowing Area of 3000㎡ (3/4 Acres)** ≈ **0.75 acre** where the owner will install the included RTK reference station and on-lawn charging station, wants app multi-zone mapping (**30** zones) without burying wire, and wants **built-in 4G** (1 year free Connect+) plus the **Anti-Theft Digital Fence** called out as exclusive to Genie 1000 & Genie 3000. Official **Maximum Slope Ability** is **24° / 45%**. Wrong machine if the measured yard is past this SKU’s marketed **0.89 acre / up to 3600 m²** ceiling, if you only need the smaller **Genie 600e** (recommended ~600 m²) or **Genie 1000** (recommended ~1000 m² / 1/4 acre), or if you cannot place the RTK base with clear sky view.

## Honest verdict

Genie 3000 is the large live-cart Genie for a **recommended ~3000 m² / 3/4 acre** wire-free yard with RTK + quad cameras, a **10 Ah** pack, **30** zones, built-in 4G, and a published **45%** slope limit. Buy it for that recommended footprint and the $899 official US price — not for a measured near-acre if you treat the **0.89-acre / 3600 m²** label as guaranteed single-pass capacity, and not if you actually need the smaller **600e** or **1000** SKU on the same PDP.

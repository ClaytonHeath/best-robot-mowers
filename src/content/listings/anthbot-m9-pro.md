Anthbot **M9 Pro** is the **$1,399** wire-free SKU on the official US M9 Pro PDP (Shopify **Default Title**, SKU **105010110**, variant id **67689364193582**, product id **15415826743598**). The same page’s HoloSense graphic titles that system **HoloSense Fusion** and labels the vision pillar **Dual AI Vision**.

This is a separate PDP from the already-listed **Anthbot M9** (`anthbot-m9`, Full-Band RTK + dual vision, no LiDAR, SKU **105010084**, $769 on 21 Oct 2026). This page is single-variant; this listing locks the **M9 Pro** cart line only.

## Who it's for

Lawns near Anthbot’s published **Recommended Mowing Area of 1000㎡ (1/4 Acres)** ≈ **0.25 acre** (buy-box bullet: mowing **up to 1000㎡ (1/4 acres)**) where the owner wants **Drop & Mow** one-tap auto-mapping, **EdgeWiz** edge mowing, and app scheduling for **up to 32 zones**, and can use **NetRTK** so a physical RTK station does not have to be installed. Official max slope on the bullets and the spec table is **45% (24°)**. Narrowest passage in the spec table is **65 cm (25.6 in)**.

Wrong machine if the measured yard is past **1000 m²**. If the yard fits that same 1000 m² figure and you do not need LiDAR, the listed **M9** is the cheaper RTK + vision card. If the yard is about **500 m²**, the unlisted **M5 Pro** (recommended **500㎡ / 1/8 acre**, cart **$1,099** on 31 Oct 2026) or the listed **M5** / **M5 LiDAR** are the smaller Anthbot cards. It is also the wrong machine if you need the listed **N8** collector or a **Genie** acreage SKU.

## Honest verdict

At **$1,399**, M9 Pro is the Anthbot card that puts **360° LiDAR**, **RTK/NetRTK**, and **vision** on a published **1000 m² (~0.25 acre)** wire-free yard, with a **45%** slope limit, a **20 cm** disc, and **one year** of 4G+NetRTK included. Buy it when that fusion is the point: shaded or obstructed ground where the listed **M9**’s RTK + vision is the worry, and the yard still fits **1000 m²**.

The catch is what the extra money does not buy. The listed [M9](/mowers/anthbot-m9/) is **$769** for the same recommended area and the same **7.9 in** class of cut, without LiDAR. The unlisted M5 Pro is **$1,099** if **500 m²** is enough. The [Dreame A3 AWD 1000](/mowers/dreame-a3-awd-1000/) listing is **$1,399.99** for the same quarter acre and cut width, with an **80%** AWD rating instead of **45%**. And although setup copy says you can skip the RTK station, the box still includes one. The US page also does not publish weight, battery size, or drive type, so do not assume the EU **5 Ah** line applies.

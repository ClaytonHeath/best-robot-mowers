## What it is

The Dreame A3 AWD Pro 2500 is the 0.62-acre SKU in Dreame Yard Care’s US A3 AWD Pro series. It is a perimeter-wire-free robot mower that maps with OmniSense™ 3.0 — official copy pairs **360° 3D LiDAR** with **binocular AI vision** — and does not use RTK or a boundary wire. Drive is all-wheel drive with hub motors (universal front wheels + off-road rear wheels).

Official US numbers published for this Pro series / SKU: working area capacity **2,500 m² / 0.62 acre** on the Pro 2500 variant, max slope **80% (38.7°)**, mowing width **40 cm / 15.8 in** dual-disc, cutting height **3–10 cm (1.2–3.9 in)**, EdgeMaster™ 2.0 disc-to-edge **< 1.2 in / < 3 cm**, weight **23.9 kg**, noise **≤ 65 dB**, waterproofing **IPX6**, and a **5 Ah** battery on the Pro 2500. Obstacle crossing is marketed at **2.2 in** (body copy also cites **2.16 in**). Dimensions in the spec table: **740 × 532 × 325 mm**.

It is not the non-Pro A3 AWD 1000/2000 (7.9 in cut, smaller capacities) and not the Pro 3500 (0.87 acre) or Pro 5000 (1.20 acre).

## Who it's for

Homeowners with up to about 0.62 acre who want a wire-free install that does not depend on an RTK antenna or Network RTK coverage, and who need AWD for slopes inside the published 80% work-area figure. App mapping is remote-control + AI auto-mapping; dual-map and multi-zone management are listed. Skip it if the lawn needs more than 0.62 acre of rated capacity (choose Pro 3500 or Pro 5000 on the same PDP), if you specifically want RTK/Network RTK, or if a smaller non-Pro A3 AWD is enough.

## What it is

The Kress EyePilot 4×4 RTKⁿ KR286 is the largest model in Kress's wire-free, four-wheel-drive EyePilot 4×4 range, sold on the official US store at kress.com/en-us. This listing covers the **KR286** only: WooCommerce product id **1298544**, SKU **KR286**. Kress names it the "EyePilot 4×4 RTKⁿ 2.5 acre robotic lawn mower with Zero Trim and integrated 4G and Premium Security".

It navigates with **RTKⁿ**: satellite corrections arrive over Wi-Fi or 4G from Kress's reference-station network, so there is no base station to install in the yard. Vision-based lawn mapping and obstacle-aware navigation handle the rest, backed by what Kress calls a redundant positioning architecture when satellite signals degrade. The drivetrain uses **four motors** with real-time torque control, an **articulated body** that keeps all four wheels grounded, and **front-wheel steering**. The **ZeroTrim** cutting system cuts close to edges, using a **9.5 in**, **4-blade** self-leveling disc paired with a larger **10 Ah** battery and **7 A** charging.

As with the two-wheel-drive [KR276](/mowers/kress-eyepilot-rtkn-kr276/), the "2.5 acre" in the product title is a 48-hour figure. Kress's key features list up to **1.5 acres in 24 hours** and **2.5 acres in 48 hours**, and the page description calls it a "1.5 acre robotic mower", so `coverageAcres` is **1.5**. It is not the ½-acre [KR282](/mowers/kress-eyepilot-4x4-rtkn-kr282/) or the 1-acre [KR284](/mowers/kress-eyepilot-4x4-rtkn-kr284/), not the two-wheel-drive EyePilot [KR272](/mowers/kress-eyepilot-rtkn-kr272/) or [KR276](/mowers/kress-eyepilot-rtkn-kr276/), and not the Mission RTKⁿ line ([KR161](/mowers/kress-rtkn-kr161/) and KR172–KR174).

## Who it's for

- Steep, bumpy or uneven lawns of about **1.5 acres per day**, or up to **2.5 acres** across a 48-hour cycle, with an **84% (≈40°)** slope rating and **2.4 in** vertical obstacle clearance.
- Owners who want four-wheel drive and front-wheel steering that turns without tearing turf.
- Yards where cleaner edges matter (**ZeroTrim**) and where burying perimeter wire or siting an RTK base station is unwelcome, provided Wi-Fi or 4G reaches the lawn.
- Not for flat multi-acre yards where the **$3,599.99** two-wheel-drive [KR276](/mowers/kress-eyepilot-rtkn-kr276/) delivers the same 1.5-acre daily capacity for $700 less, or steep 1-acre yards where the **$3,599.99** [KR284](/mowers/kress-eyepilot-4x4-rtkn-kr284/) already fits.

## Honest verdict

At **$4,299.99** (26 Oct 2026), the KR286 sits at the top of Kress's EyePilot 4×4 range. Compared with the 1-acre [KR284](/mowers/kress-eyepilot-4x4-rtkn-kr284/) (**$3,599.99**), paying $700 more enlarges the battery from 6 Ah to **10 Ah**, bumps charging current from 5 A to **7 A**, and raises mowing capacity to **1.5 acres in 24 hours** (**2.5 acres in 48 hours**), while keeping the **9.5 in 4-blade** deck, **84%** four-wheel-drive chassis, **ZeroTrim** edging, **IPX6** and built-in **4G** with no ongoing fee.

The catch is the headline acreage and the $700 premium. That "2.5 acre" badge relies on a two-day cycle, and the deck is still **9.5 in** wide on a **44.53 lb** chassis. If your 1.5-acre lawn has gentle slopes within **40%**, the two-wheel-drive [KR276](/mowers/kress-eyepilot-rtkn-kr276/) matches the KR286's 1.5-acre daily capacity and 9.5 in 4-blade deck for **$3,599.99**. If your steep lawn is an acre or less, the [KR284](/mowers/kress-eyepilot-4x4-rtkn-kr284/) also saves $700. Buy the KR286 when you have more than an acre of genuinely steep or rough ground and want wire-free, antenna-free AWD coverage.

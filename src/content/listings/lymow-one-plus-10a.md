## What it is

The Lymow One Plus is a wire-free robot mower on **crawler tracks** with **two rotary mulching blades**. It navigates with **RTK satellite positioning plus VSLAM** camera mapping. This listing is the US **10A** version only: Shopify variant **47332598743275**, SKU **LM1PLUSUS10A**, titled "10A | 1.73 Acres/Day".

It is the same chassis as the listed [Lymow One Plus 5A](https://www.bestlawnrobots.com/mowers/lymow-one-plus-5a/) (`lymow-one-plus-5a`). Both share one spec table and the same 15 Ah battery. The table splits them only on charging: the 10A charges from 10% to 90% in **90 minutes** at **40.5 V**; the 5A takes **150 minutes** at **39 V**. The variant names put the 10A at **1.73 acres per day** and the 5A at **1.1 acres per day**. It is not the earlier Lymow One. Lymow says the One and One Plus blades and batteries are not interchangeable.

From the official US spec table (3 Oct 2026):

- Navigation: **RTK + VSLAM**; RTK coverage radius up to **3,200 ft**
- Without RTK: **0.025–0.037 acre**, up to **10 minutes**
- Map storage: **15 acres**; multi-zone management: **up to 80 zones**
- Connectivity: **Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and 4G**
- Obstacle avoidance: spec table text is "Al Vision + 5 ultrasonic sensors + 2 Hall sensors" (marketing copy says AI vision); cliff detection: 2 Hall sensors
- Slope: **up to 45° (100% incline)**; obstacle crossing height: **2.8 in** (lab figure)
- Blades: rotary mulching blades; blade speed **3,000–6,000 RPM** (lab figure)
- Cutting height: **1.2–4.0 in**; cutting width: **16 in**
- Mowing speed: **1.0–3.3 ft/s**
- Blade motor: **680 W** rated, **1,785 W** peak
- Coverage in the table: **0.23 acre per hour**, **0.57 acre per charge**, **1.73 acres per day**
- Battery: **LiFePO₄, 15,000 mAh (15 Ah)**, 35.2 V rated, **3 hours** maximum runtime, **2,000 charge cycles** (lab figure)
- Waterproofing: **IPX6**
- Weight: **78.5 lb ±1 lb**; dimensions: **29.5 × 23.6 × 12.6 in**

## Who it's for

This 10A version fits a lawn that is steep, rooty, or rough enough to want tracks rated for **45° (100%)** and a **2.8-inch** obstacle step, and that is large enough that the **1.1-acre-per-day** 5A rating is the wrong daily figure. Lymow also pitches the dual rotary blades at tall or thick grass and at mulching fallen leaves.

Plan the **RTK reference station** before you buy. It is in the box, but the mower barely works without it: Lymow lists only **0.025–0.037 acre**, or about **10 minutes**, of mowing without RTK. The station can go on a pole or a wall, and it needs power. The charging station needs power too. There is **no boundary wire**. You map with a phone and draw virtual boundaries in the app.

Lymow does not publish a maximum lawn size. `coverageAcres` uses the **1.73 acres per day** figure from this variant's name and from the spec table. The page footnotes that 1.73 as a laboratory maximum on flat ground, full charge, continuous mowing, moderate grass height, and no docking interruptions. The **15-acre** figure is map storage, not coverage. If the lawn is well under about an acre a day, the [5A](https://www.bestlawnrobots.com/mowers/lymow-one-plus-5a/) (`lymow-one-plus-5a`) is the same machine at a lower cart price. Skip the One Plus if the yard is small and flat and you do not want to site an RTK station or move a **78.5 lb** machine.

## Standout details

- **What the 10A changes:** charge time **90 minutes** (10% to 90%) at **40.5 V**, versus **150 minutes** at **39 V** on the 5A, and a variant rating of **1.73 acres per day** versus **1.1**. Chassis, battery, cut, slope and navigation are shared.
- **Drive and slope:** heavy-duty tracks, rated for **45° (100%)** and for crossing **2.8-inch** obstacles (lab). Lymow names tree roots, branches, steep slopes and gravel.
- **Cutting:** two rotary blades Lymow describes as SK5 tool steel (50 HRC), a **16-inch** cut and a floating deck. Height **1.2–4.0 in**, with a different height per zone. Clippings leave by a single side discharge. The page says the deck lifts for cleaning and that the blades mulch leaves.
- **Navigation:** RTK + VSLAM, **3,200 ft** RTK radius, **15 acres** of map storage, up to **80 zones**, plus vision and ultrasonic obstacle avoidance. It returns to charge on a low battery or rain, then resumes.
- **Battery:** **15 Ah LiFePO₄**, up to **3 hours** per charge, **2,000** cycles (lab). The 10A charger is the reason this SKU exists.
- **Build:** A380 aluminum frame, **IPX6**, **78.5 lb ±1 lb**. No noise figure is published, so `noiseDb` is omitted.
- **Security and connectivity:** geo-fence alerts, a device lock and live GPS tracking. Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and 4G. The same store sells Lymow 4G service plans ($22.90 for 6 months, $34.90 for 12 months). The product page does not say whether any data period is included.
- **In the box:** mower, charging station, charging-station adaptor, 10 m charging-station extension cable, 4 ground stakes, RTK reference station, radio antenna, 2 mounting poles, wall-mount bracket, RTK ground stake, 4 expansion bolts, RTK power adaptor, 2 × 5 m RTK extension cables, user manual and quick start guide.
- **Purchase terms on this date:** free shipping; estimated US ship time **1–3 business days**; **3-year** limited warranty; **30-day** returns. US sales tax is added at checkout (Lymow FAQ). Cart price **$2,799** against compare-at **$3,199**. A $50 subscribe coupon is offered and was not applied.

## Honest verdict

At **$2,799** (compare-at **$3,199**), the 10A is the faster-charging One Plus: **90 minutes** from 10% to 90%, and a **1.73-acre-per-day** rating, on tracks rated for **100% (45°)** slopes, with a **16-inch** dual-blade cut and no boundary wire. What you still give up is the same as the [5A](https://www.bestlawnrobots.com/mowers/lymow-one-plus-5a/) (`lymow-one-plus-5a`): you have to site and power the included RTK station, and the machine weighs **78.5 lb**.

The 5A cart on the same day is **$2,499** (compare-at **$2,999**), **$300** less, for a **150-minute** charge and a **1.1-acre-per-day** name. Buy the 10A if the lawn actually needs the higher daily figure. If it does not, the listed 5A is the same mower for less. The published 5A page still shows the older **$2,999 / $3,199** pair from 28 Sep 2026; those cart prices have moved.

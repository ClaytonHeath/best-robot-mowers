## What it is

The Raccoon 2 SE is RoboUP's entry-level wire-free robot mower, sold on the official RoboUP US store at us.iroboup.com. This listing is the **mower-only Raccoon 2 SE** configuration: Shopify product **10274819146022**, variant **52045493076262**, SKU **Raccoon2SE-RobotLawnMower** (GTIN 6976332010151).

It navigates by camera. The US spec table lists positioning and navigation as **vSLAM + INS** (visual mapping plus an inertial sensor) and obstacle avoidance as **AI camera recognition plus a bumper**. RoboUP's pitch is "No wires, no RTK, no app required for simple lawns": no perimeter wire and no RTK reference station. The mower uses AI vision to tell lawn from non-lawn. For open lawns without clear borders, such as shared grass, RoboUP recommends optional boundary stakes placed about 40 cm (15.7 in) apart. These are ground stakes, not a wire.

This is a different machine from the listed [roboup-tm01-t1200pro](https://us.iroboup.com/products/roboup-tm01-t1200-pro-robot-lawn-mower?variant=52962413871398). The T1200Pro adds RTK, is rated for 0.37 acre and 45% slopes, and has a 1.2–2.4 in height range.

## Who it's for

Owners of small, simple lawns up to **0.15 acre** whose grass is bordered by paths, beds, fences, or walls the camera can see. It suits buyers who want the lowest price for a wire-free mower and don't want to site an RTK antenna. RoboUP says it is tested under trees and in shade, where satellite-based mowers can struggle. For lawns under **80 m² (861 sq ft)** you can skip mapping, place the mower, and start it.

Skip it if the lawn is over 0.15 acre, has slopes steeper than **36% (20°)**, or runs into a neighbor's grass with no visible edge and you don't want to set out stakes. If you want straight stripes across a whole lawn, note that the FAQ says it mows in random patterns by default. It does offer systematic L-pattern mowing after mapping, and parallel lines in target areas under 80 m².

## Standout details

- **Coverage:** Max mowing size **0.15 acre** on the US spec table. The product header says "for simple, well-defined small lawns up to 0.15 acre."
- **Navigation:** vSLAM + INS, with automatic or manual mapping and remote control for tricky areas. You can choose random mowing or systematic L-pattern mowing, which RoboUP says is up to 1.6x more efficient. It handles up to **three zones**, even when separated by stone paths or driveways.
- **Slope:** **20° (36%)** max slope on the spec table and in the FAQ. RoboUP does not publish a drive type.
- **Cut:** **200 mm (7.9 in)** floating cutting disc with 3 blades, and **electric height adjustment from 30–80 mm (1.2–3.1 in)** set in the app. Ride-on-edge cutting and manual spot cutting are included. The FAQ says it works best on grass up to 3.15 in and can travel through grass up to 4.7 in.
- **Power:** 5 Ah battery, about **150 min** of mowing per charge (typical) and **70 min** charging. It recharges automatically.
- **Build:** **11.5 kg (25.4 lb)**, **IPX6**, **≤56 dBA**, off-road tires, lift and tilt alarm, emergency stop. Dimensions are 617 × 408 × 288 mm (24.3 × 16.1 × 11.3 in).
- **Connectivity:** Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz) and Bluetooth. RoboUP says the app isn't required for simple lawns.

## Honest verdict

For a small lawn with clear borders, this is the cheapest wire-free mower on the site: **$579** on sale from $699, with no RTK pole, no wire, electric height adjustment, IPX6, and a 150-minute run time. The published limits are a 0.15-acre rating, a 36% (20°) slope, a 7.9 in cut, and camera-based boundaries that need stakes on open lawns. Against its sibling, the listed [roboup-tm01-t1200pro](https://us.iroboup.com/products/roboup-tm01-t1200-pro-robot-lawn-mower?variant=52962413871398) was $799 on 7 Oct 2026. For $220 more it adds RTK, a 0.37-acre rating, and 45% slopes. The listed [eufy-e15](https://www.eufy.com/products/t28801a1?variant=44697729269946) is also camera-only, but it lists at $1,799.99 for a 0.2-acre rating. Buy the Raccoon 2 SE if the lawn really is small and bordered. Otherwise, the extra coverage and RTK on the T1200Pro is worth the step up.

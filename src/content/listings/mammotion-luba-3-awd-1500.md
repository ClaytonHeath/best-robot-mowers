## What it is

LUBA 3 AWD is Mammotion’s 2026 all-wheel-drive robot mower. Navigation is what Mammotion calls Tri-Fusion: 360° LiDAR, dual-camera AI vision, and (on larger SKUs) NetRTK via Mammotion iNavi. The **1500** is the smallest US model, rated for **0.37 acre (1,500 m²)**.

On this SKU, Mammotion is explicit: high-performance 360° LiDAR covers the 0.37-acre lawn **without RTK / NetRTK**. The 1500/1500H box does not include an RTK reference station. Larger 3000/5000/10000 models add NetRTK (and, in some regions, a physical RTK station).

## Who it's for

Yards that are modest in area but awkward in terrain — slopes, trees, furniture, narrow gaps. Official slope rating for the AWD series is **80% (38.6°)**. It is not the right listing if you have a 1-acre lawn; buy a larger LUBA 3 SKU, or another brand’s acre-class machine.

## Standout details

- **Coverage:** 0.37 acre on the 1500. Do not confuse this with the 1.25-acre 5000 or 2.5-acre 10000 in the same family.
- **Navigation:** 360° × 59° LiDAR, up to 70 m range, 200,000 points/second on the series page, plus dual-camera AI vision. NetRTK is a larger-SKU feature, not this one.
- **Drive:** AWD is the LUBA point of difference. 80% slope is the published series figure.
- **Cut:** 15.7 in (400 mm) dual cutting discs in the LUBA 3 series documentation. Standard cutting height 1.0–2.7 in; High (H) version 2.2–4.0 in.
- **Build:** 41 lb listed on the US product page. IPX6 (hose-washable, not IP66). Noise listed as ≤70 dB.
- **Connectivity:** Built-in 4G with three years included; Mammotion’s FAQ says a subscription is required after that. Live streaming is advertised for yard monitoring.
- **Gaps:** Series page says it fits passages as narrow as 70 cm (27.6 in).

Sources: [LUBA 3 AWD US product page](https://us.mammotion.com/products/luba-3-awd-robot-lawn-mower), checked 26 Aug 2026. Battery runtime of “up to 215 minutes / 15 Ah” is a series headline; it is **not** broken out per SKU on that page, so it is omitted here.

## Honest verdict

If you actually have ~0.37 acre and hills, the 1500 is a coherent product: LiDAR plus AWD, no perimeter wire, no RTK pole. If you have a bigger lawn, this SKU’s coverage number is the whole story — step up a model, or look at Navimow’s acre-class RTK mowers. Also budget for 4G after year three, and treat IPX6 as “hose the deck,” not a submarine rating.

## What it is

The MOVA LiDAX Ultra 1000 is the **0.25-acre** SKU in MOVA’s US LiDAX Ultra (non-AWD) pair. This listing is locked to US variant **47255944626412**, SKU **010901AA000182**, barcode **850071220025**, titled **MOVA LIDAX Ultra 1000 Lawn Mower | 0.25 acre**. It is not the 0.5-acre Ultra 2000, not LiDAX Ultra 2000 AWD, and not a Dreame A3 AWD model.

Navigation is **UltraView™ 2.0**: official US copy pairs **360° 3D LiDAR** with **AI vision** for RTK-free, perimeter-wire-free mapping (remote control + AI auto-mapping). Drive is **rear-wheel drive** with off-road wheels. Official US comparison table for this SKU: max mapping area **0.25 acre**, dimensions **26.2 × 17.5 × 10.7"**, weight **30.2 lbs**, cutting width **7.9"**, cutting height **1.2"–3.9"**, UltraTrim 1.0 edge distance **< 2"**, max climbing **45% (24°)**, vertical passing **≤1.6"**, battery **4 Ah**, charge **60 min**, mowing time per charge **60–80 min**, waterproofing **IPX6**, and TrueGuard™ listed **Y**.

## Who it's for

Buy this cart if the mowable area is around or under **0.25 acre**, you want a wire-free LiDAR install without an RTK station, and slopes stay inside the published **45%** work-area figure on RWD. Dual-map and multi-zone management (marketing: up to **150** zones) cover split front/back layouts when you accept docking and resume cycles on the **4 Ah** pack.

Skip it if you need **0.5 acre** rated capacity (Ultra 2000 on the same store), **AWD / 80%** slopes or a **15.8"** dual-disc deck (Ultra AWD line), or if you expected Dreame Yard Care branding — that is a different brand and catalog.

## Honest verdict

If the yard fits **0.25 acre**, you want **LiDAR + vision** without RTK hardware, and **45% RWD** is enough slope, the locked Ultra 1000 cart at **$999** ($1,299 compare-at) is the small Ultra.

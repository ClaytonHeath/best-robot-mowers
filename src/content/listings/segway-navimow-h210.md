## What it is

The Segway Navimow H210 is the smaller of the two **H2 series** robot mowers sold on Navimow's official US store at navimow.com. This listing covers the **H210** variant only: Shopify product **8367261089929**, variant **45014291382409** ("Navimow H210 | 0.25 acre"), SKU **AA.12.05.01.0002**. It is not the **H220** (0.5 acre, $2,199) on the same page.

Navimow calls the navigation **EFLS™ (LiDAR + NRTK + Vision)**. A built-in solid-state LiDAR, Network RTK and a camera work together, so there is **no boundary wire and no RTK antenna** to install. Where Network RTK coverage is available, you connect through the Navimow app instead of mounting a local antenna. The page has a coverage checker.

From the **Navimow H2 US user manual** linked from the product page (Specifications table, H210 column, checked **16 Oct 2026**):

- Recommended mowing area: **about 0.25 acre**
- Max incline **inside the working area: 45%** (24°). Max incline **at the boundary: 25%**.
- Cutting width: **8.66 in**. Cutting height: **2–4 in**. The page lists a **low-cutting kit** in the box.
- Typical mowing time per full charge: **about 110 min**. Charging time: **about 70 min**. Area per hour: **2,045 ft²**.
- Battery: **5.1 Ah / 110 Wh**, 21.6 V lithium-ion
- Net weight: **34.5 lbs** (battery included). Size: **24.6 × 17.5 × 11.6 in**.
- Noise: **60 dB(A)** sound power (LWA) and **52 dB(A)** sound pressure (LpA)
- IP rating: **IP66** for the mower and charging station, **IP67** for the power supply
- Warranty in the US manual: **3 years** on the H210/H220, **2 years** on battery packs and power adapters

`coverageAcres` is the **0.25-acre** recommended mowing area. The page's "up to 0.93 acre per day" figure is footnoted as an H220 result, so it does not apply to this variant.

## Who it's for

A **lawn of up to about a quarter acre** that is awkward for single-sensor mowers: areas **under trees**, narrow paths, open ground, or mowing **at night**. Navimow's EFLS™ copy names all of these. Setup is "drop and mow": you place the charging station, and the mower maps the yard itself in the app. Nothing is buried and there's no antenna pole.

Check two things before buying. Network RTK depends on **cellular coverage**, and the page has a coverage checker. Also, the **45%** rating applies inside the work area. The manual caps the **boundary at 25%**.

Skip it if the lawn is well over a quarter acre (the H220 covers about half an acre on the same page), or if the yard needs AWD and an 80%-class slope rating.

- **Navigation:** EFLS™ fuses a built-in **solid-state LiDAR** with **Network RTK** and **vision**. Navimow quotes about **200,000 points per second** and a **230 ft** detection range.
- **Obstacles:** the page says it detects objects down to **0.4 in** and recognizes **200+ types**, from toys and swings to moving animals.

## Honest verdict

At **$1,799**, the H210 is the quarter-acre H2. It has the same LiDAR + network RTK + vision navigation, **8.66-inch** cut, **2–4 in** height, **IP66**, **60 dB(A)** and 45% / 25% slope limits as the listed [segway-navimow-h220](https://navimow.com/products/navimow-h2-robot-lawn-mower?variant=45014291415177). It is the same 24.6 × 17.5 × 11.6 in body.

**How it differs from the H220:**

| | H210 | H220 |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $1,799 | $2,199 ($400 more) |
| Recommended area | about 0.25 acre | about 0.5 acre |
| Battery | 5.1 Ah / 110 Wh | 10 Ah / 216 Wh |
| Runtime per charge | about 110 min | about 220 min |
| Charge time | about 70 min | about 120 min |
| Area per hour | 2,045 ft² | 2,637 ft² |
| Weight | 34.5 lb | 35.82 lb |

The limits are the same for both. Neither is AWD. Both are rated for **45%** inside the work area and **25%** at the boundary. Network RTK and the 4G features rely on cellular service. The standard deck starts at **2 in**, so you fit the low-cutting kit to go shorter. Buy the H210 if your lawn is a tricky quarter acre with moderate slopes. If it's closer to half an acre, the H220 is the better fit.

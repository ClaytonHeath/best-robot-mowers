## What it is

The eufy Robot Lawn Mower E15 is a wire-free robot mower that navigates with cameras only, sold on eufy's official US store at eufy.com. This listing covers the **Gray E15**: Shopify product **8071515832506**, variant **44697729269946**, SKU **T28801A1** (GTIN 194644231156).

## Who it's for

The E15 fits a **small, simple, flat lawn**: under 8,700 sq ft of grass, no slope steeper than 18°, and no ruts or steps over about an inch. It suits a buyer who doesn't want to bury wire or find a sky-clear spot for an RTK antenna. eufy's pitch is that you place the base, open the app, and the mower maps the yard itself. The app handles multiple zones, virtual walls, and remote scheduling of time, path, height, and speed.

Check your grass type before buying. eufy's own buyer checklist rules out **St. Augustine and dense Zoysia**, which are common in much of the US South. The page also says to subscribe to **4G data in the app** if Wi-Fi doesn't cover the whole yard.

Skip it if the lawn is over 0.2 acre, hilly, or bumpy, or if you mow St. Augustine or dense Zoysia. Those buyers should look at an RTK, LiDAR, or AWD machine.

## Honest verdict

At **$1,299.99** in the cart (list **$1,799.99**, with eufy's own $500 code applied automatically), the E15 is a camera-only robot mower for **0.2 acre** or less. Nothing goes in the ground and there is no RTK antenna to place. It has an **8-inch** cut, **1–3 in** height, **IPX6**, **< 56 dB**, and GPS/4G anti-theft. For a small, flat, open yard, that is a simple setup.

The limits are the fine print eufy prints itself. The maximum is **0.2 acre** and the slope rating is **under 18°**. The ground has to be mostly flat, with bumps under **1.2 in**. Grass must be under **3.5 in**, and **St. Augustine and dense Zoysia** are excluded. Each charge covers only about **100–150 m²**, so it mows in short cycles. The $1,299.99 price also depends on the coupon staying live. Buy it if the lawn really is small, level, and a compatible grass. Otherwise, a machine with RTK or LiDAR and a stronger slope rating will fit better.

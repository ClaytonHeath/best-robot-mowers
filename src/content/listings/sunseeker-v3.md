## What it is

The Sunseeker V3 is a wire-free robot mower that navigates by camera, sold on Sunseeker's official US store, shopsunseekertech.com, under the name "V3 Wireless AI Robot Lawn Mower for Small Yards | ReadyGo". This listing is Shopify product **9845335851325**, variant **49970131894589**, SKU **SKRMV3**.

Navigation is **Vision AI** using a dual-lens 3D camera. Sunseeker says the mower reads clear lawn edges, recognizes more than 200 obstacle types, and adjusts its steering within 3 seconds. There's **no boundary wire and no RTK base station**. For open or unclear edges, the V3 uses **magnetic strips**, and two come in the box. You draw virtual boundaries, no-go zones and channels in the app, and Sunseeker's "ReadyGo" pitch is plug in, press start, and mow.

**In the box (from the official "What's in the box?" image on the US page):**
- the robotic lawn mower and a charging station
- a charger
- 2 magnetic strips, 2 magnetic tags, 4 magnetic strip connectors and 20 magnetic strip pegs
- 12 nails
- blades ×3+6 and screws ×3+6
- manuals

## Who it's for

Owners of **small, simple lawns up to 0.15 acre** with clear edges and straightforward layouts. That's how Sunseeker itself describes the V3, and it lists "large lawns (above 0.15 acre)" and "slopes too steep" as poor fits. The **42% (22°)** slope rating is generous for this class, and the mower handles up to **100 zones**.

Look elsewhere if your lawn is bigger than 0.15 acre, has soft or blurry edges you don't want to mark with magnetic strips, or needs mowing after dark. Sunseeker recommends daylight use and a lawn kept clear of toys, branches and debris.

## Honest verdict

For a small lawn with clear edges, the V3 does the job at **$799.99**. You get camera navigation with no wire or antenna, a 42% slope rating, 55 dB operation and 100 zones, and magnetic strips are included for the tricky edges. It isn't the cheapest small-yard option we list:
- The [roboup-raccoon-2-se](https://us.iroboup.com/products/roboup-raccoon-2-se-robot-mower?variant=52045493076262) is also vision-only and rated 0.15 acre, for $579 (back in stock on 29 Oct 2026 after being sold out on 28 Oct 2026), and climbs only 36%.
- The [anthbot-genie](https://www.anthbot.com/products/genie-robot-lawn-mower) costs $699, also covers 0.15 acre, and adds RTK + vision with a 45% rating.
- The camera-only [eufy-e15](https://www.eufy.com/products/t28801a1?variant=44697729269946) covers a bit more (0.2 acre) but lists at $1,799.99 and is rated for slopes under 18°.

The limits are the 0.15-acre rating, a camera system Sunseeker recommends for daylight and tidy lawns, and a US page with no cutting width, height range, weight or battery figures. The sale price also moves: it was $599.99 two days earlier. Buy it if your lawn is small, simple and sloped. Wait for a lower price if you're not in a hurry, and choose the Raccoon 2 SE if price matters most.
